Gundam comes to the Outlands March 10.

Fight in the Gundam universe and wield iconic weapons like the Buster Rifle, unleash Gundam Wildcard abilities, and fight on a Broken Moon filled with destroyed Mobile Suits and Gundam Wing statues.

## WILDCARD: GUNDAM TAKEOVER**{#**gundam-takeover**}**

![In-game screenshot of a Gundam in a valley on the Broken moon map. There are explosions going off on the surface of the planet in the background.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/4jB48uPzzDahxuwLKVJQKN/364da8da0c2c5714e8831f692a75a3d0/APEX_Legends_S28_Screenshots_Beamstick_GunplaScenicsA_v03.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.7458333333333333,yPosition=0.4847222222222222)

Drop into Wildcard and earn your wings. Broken Moon transforms into a battlefield for the event, with destroyed Mobile Suits scattered across the terrain, massive climbable Wing Gundam statues, and ships battling overhead. 

![In-game screenshot of a Gundam aiming a Gundam-themed Kraber on the Broken Moon map.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/4tfov7uPtcytNpapnrWnZ9/9dd40d8637f9bb9c1399ab4115e88375/vlcsnap-2026-02-24-13h24m26s518.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.15989583333333332,yPosition=0.4439814814814815)

Grab the Buster Rifle from Care Packages and launch skyward for aerial recon, scan through walls to mark every enemy, and fire laser beams that cut through cover and explode on impact.

![In-game screenshot of 2 Gundam characters shooting at each other.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/1GJ7zNlveXQAgh5ux1fson/212fe273a3290f2fec6998a50c90d410/APEX_Legends_S28_Screenshots_Beamstick_BitStavesA_v04.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.334375,yPosition=0.2523148148148148)

Use Bit Staves as shields to absorb damage while you push, or switch to Drone Command and send them out to patrol, scan, and attack anyone who dares trespass in your territory.

![In-game screenshot of a Gundam shooting at another Gundam, who's using a Epyon's Lash back at them. Another Gundam is flying in the air behind both of them shooting.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/6K5cBkdcshxsAxQ0gXaGq2/a11b444d03dab8014a1265be8c8fd592/APEX_Legends_S28_Screenshots_Beamstick_WildCardA_v03.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.26276041666666666,yPosition=0.40370370370370373)

Unleash lethal Gundam abilities with New Wild Cards, including: 

- **Epyon&#39;s Lash:** Adds a magnetic melee pull Tactical ability, and more.
- **Heavyarms Salvo:** Marks enemies for air support missile strikes after landing multiple hits.
- **Zero Sacrifice:** Adds the option to trigger a self-destruct explosion on knock, taking nearby enemies with you.
- **Zero Rebirth:** Enter the Void when knocked, then exit with 1 HP,  temporary invulnerability and health regen.

## PATCH NOTES: RECENT UPDATES &amp; FIXES**{#**patch-notes**}**

Here are some updates and fixes from our most recent patches:

- Fixed an edge case crash impacting Trios and Duos
- Server stability improvements
- Fixed a hole where players were able to fall through on Broken Moon in BR and Wildcard
- Completing Road to Ranked Challenges now correctly unlock Ranked
- Fixed a situation where players would lose access to main tabs on home screens or their currencies would display incorrectly
- &quot;Riptide Predator&quot; Peacekeeper Skin draw speed now matches all other weapons
- Choosing a controller perk now grants that Legend the ability to reinforce Hardlight as intended
- Players will no longer spawn over water in Wildcard
- Adjusted Ranked lobbies to provide either a Dropship or Drop Zone experiences based on that lobby&#39;s players&#39; rank composition 

    - All matches with 10+ Diamond players OR 1+ Master/Predator player will have Drop Zones
    - Players may experience a mixture of Dropship and Drop Zone matches, particularly for Platinum players or those squadding up with Diamond+ players, as we work out the thresholds
    - If your pending lobby will have Drop Zones you will see “RANKED - DROP ZONES” displayed on the match loading screen
    - When loading into a Drop Zones match, the Rank Tier Display will show each player&#39;s individual Rank Tier (Dropship matches will keep the current &#39;Squad&#39; version of the Rank Tier Distribution)
