# Apex Legends™: Takeover Twitch Drops


Tune in to select Twitch streams between February 11-17, 2025, to earn cosmetics while watching your favorite streamers dominate. 

<u>[Instructions on how to link your EA account can be found here](https://help.ea.com/en/help/account/link-your-ea-account-to-twitch/)</u>.  
## LET THE DROPS BEGIN 

Once your account is set up, sign into your Twitch account and watch the folks we’ve designated for drops each week. 

- You need to have an EA account
- Twitch Drops are available on PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, and PC
- Players will receive Twitch Drop rewards based on how much they watch
- You’ll see your reward in-game within 24 hours of the event the next time you log on
