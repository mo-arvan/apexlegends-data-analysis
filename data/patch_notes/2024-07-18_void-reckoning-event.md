# Stand Steadfast in the Void Reckoning Event


[![Apex Legends: Void Reckoning Event Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=veMD9nuQ1Xk)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/6t6xL7rcuGkn3CVcQkqyx2/89607af84163ea58a542be16c4693e60/void-reckoning-ce-trailer-thumbnail.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.64609375,yPosition=0.3736111111111111)(Youtube)(void-reckoning-ce-trailer-thumbnail)

The invasion is upon us, Legends! Join the vanguard in the Void Reckoning Event and suit up for an all-out brawl in Big TDM, our first-ever 12v12 deathmatch. Slay your foes and earn Void Points in both BR and Big TDM to unlock new cosmetics and more in the Rewards Shop. Pull from a pool of 22 knight-themed cosmetics, including the “Crestfallen Stars” Reactive Havoc, and earn milestone rewards like Newcastle’s Legendary “Galactic Garrison” as you complete your collection.
## NEW LIMITED-TIME MODE: BIG TDM

What’s that old saying? Go big or go home? Well this Team Deathmatch variant is definitely not going home. Join 23 other players in a 12 v 12 brawl across 5 maps. Big TDM brings improved health regeneration and an increased score limit of 60. Pick your fav Legend and go do big things.
## MILESTONE EVENT ITEMS

Unlock 22 galant limited-time cosmetics to proceed through the event and unlock milestone rewards! Each Void Reckoning item received during the event counts towards these bonus rewards that you’ll automatically unlock after earning 5, 12, and 17 items.

All 22 items, including the “Crestfallen Stars” Reactive Havoc, can be acquired via Void Reckoning Event Packs†—and there are no duplicates of event items in these packs. Just looking for the event Legend skins? You can pick those up directly in the Shop. Note: each event pack contains one event item and two standard items.
## REWARD SHOP

Earn Void Points by completing daily event challenges, then head to the Void Reckoning Reward Shop to choose your own rewards and see your progress with event badges. The Reward Shop and offerings rotate weekly. 

A limited amount of Void Points can be earned per day by completing challenges and they refresh daily.

![Apex Legends Void Reckoning event screen showing weapon rewards, event currency, and progression track with a featured purple-and-blue energy gun.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/XqPWgL0kEaq7UFtwFXIne/a8678b322744e211143857de2936a5a8/Void_Reckoning_Reward_Shop_002_v1.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)
## STORE TAB

Each of our chivalrous Legend skins, like Wraith’s “Void Vanguard” and Caustic’s “Noxious Knight,” are available for direct purchase during the event. Collect the remainder of the Void Reckoning items to auto-unlock final milestone rewards like Newcastle’s Legendary “Galactic Garrison” during the event. Milestone rewards will be available for AC in the store for a limited time following the conclusion of the Void Reckoning event.

![Apex Legends store promo featuring five skins available from July 23–August 6: Wraith, Horizon, Ash, Caustic, and Mirage, each priced at 1,800 coins.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/5Y2zvg1c4MdzOOIy6KK5aS/2d635aa9f8a17f24440896e29b90e996/Void_Reckoning_Store_Offers_v1.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)
