# Apex Legends™: Community Handcrafts/Builds Spotlight


![Spotlight della Comunità Apex per Artigianato e Costruzioni con le costruzioni in mattoni di Bricklefront, le creazioni all'uncinetto di Nessiemakes, le stampe 3D di Shiroto e gli amici infeltriti di Kiuichii.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/3zS6SaZuBuERCseTNzEZOJ/1b9435d3cee55a81ea50d8b431cb9345/S24__Community_Spotlights__BuildsHandcrafts.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.69150467962563,yPosition=0.4849648112603967)

Is it really an Apex Legends anniversary season if there isn’t a community spotlight? Alongside our ongoing Creator Commissioners and their community challenges, we’re also shining a light on some community leaders from a plethora of creative genres. These folks aren’t just legendary in The Outlands, but IRL too. 

Today’s spotlight showcases the wide variety of leaders in the handcraft and build spaces in the Apex Legends community.
## BRICKLEFRONT

![Apex Community Spotlight for Bricklefront showcasing their brick built Legends, Heirlooms, Flatline, and additional Apex weapons.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/1xwdnkwNYQRECN9as6XnRs/b28ac3b903a25168069997a41b41095d/S24_-_Spotlight_1_-_Bricklefront.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)

Find them on: <u>[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/bricklefront/)</u>

Bricklefront is a creator that utilizes a material known to many: building bricks. His creations started out as physical builds before branching into creating and sharing digital builds from across franchises. From the Outlands, Bricklefront has recreated Legends, their Heirlooms, and an arsenal of Apex Legends weaponry including the Flatline utilizing bricks.

**Favorite Piece:** “The Flatline replica, because it’s the most realistic and most difficult one that I have built, it took me more than 12 hours of work to build it, and I&#39;m really happy with the result.”

**Fun Fact:** “I was initially building with real LEGO pieces, but I realized I didn’t always have the parts I wanted in my collection. I decided to learn how to build digitally, and I haven&#39;t stopped building on the computer since.” 
## KIUICHII 

![Apex Community Spotlight for Kiuichii showcasing their felted art pieces with Wattson and Nessie, Nessie's birthday party, and Wraith and Wattson floating on a Nessie.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/2StUqp2zKreVwMW2IZSOvO/598011b0d34c8793843cd42c92466f21/S24_-_Spotlight_1_-__Kiuichii.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)

Find them on: <u>[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/kiuichii/)</u> | <u>[Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/kiuichii)</u> | <u>[X/Twitter](https://x.com/kiuichii)</u>

Kiuichii is an artist that brings her love for comfy and cozy directly into their art. Her craft begins with needling felt into creations like Wraith, Wattson, and Nessie, then they spin them into unique pieces that become prints, stickers, and more. 

**Fun Fact: “**I love to make soft and playful art. I also love being a problem causing Wattson main! Combining the two brings me so much joy! I hope it can bring you joy as well.” 
## NESSIEMAKES 

![Apex Community Spotlight for Nessiemakes showcasing their crocheted snow Nessie, Alter, Conduit, and Loba.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/1NKwbWMeMzKxfd2O2fJ6z8/6dd6b31c8b0b631ce37106395ef0d969/S24_-_Spotlight_1_-_Nessiemakes.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)

Find them on: <u>[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/nessiemakesthings)</u> | <u>[X/Twitter](https://x.com/Nessiemakes)</u>

Nessiemakes is a UK-based creator that brings plushies to life via crochet. Each one starts by mapping out a pattern and sourcing materials before they take form via her handcraft. She’s reimagined a plethora of Legends and Nessies alike in this unique style and has been featured at ALGS events.

**Favorite Piece:** “My favourite piece I&#39;ve done was designing my own Nessie, as I&#39;m also a big fan of Nessies and now I get to make different variations of them. :)”

**Fun Fact:** “I started crocheting at the start of 2023, and 6 months later I started designing my first Apex plushie design, which was Wattson!” 
## SHIROTO

![Apex Community Spotlight for Shiroto showcasing their handmade, 3D printed Apex recreations including the Nemesis, Flatline, Mozambique, and collection of Raptor's Claws.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/5J5y1I1Qoy7ziNaP8npQZv/4d90cf5201c846baf8278cb7bf47aa37/S24_-_Spotlight_1_-_shiroto.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)

Find them on: <u>[X/Twitter](https://x.com/shiroto301)</u> | <u>[Youtube](http://www.youtube.com/@shiroto.ch3d922)</u>

Shiroto is an accomplished sculptor who has recreated many weapons and Heirlooms, with their work being featured in the Apex Legends Museum pop-up in Japan. Each piece begins as a model in 3DCAD before they take form via 3D printing, building, and final touches. More recently, their work was featured at ALGS Year 4 Champs in Sapporo.

**Favorite Piece:** “My favorite work is the Flatline. Everything is cool, that&#39;s all I can say.”

**Fun Fact:** “I use a 3D printer to model weapons and items. I do everything from modeling to painting. Some weapons are slightly movable.”
