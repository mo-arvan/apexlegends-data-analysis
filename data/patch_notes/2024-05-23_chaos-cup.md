# Apex Legends™ Chaos Cup UK


![chaos-cup-3840x2160](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/3Ob1NrfYl93NyuHLxSitnj/dab3c47a511f2933f7da9ea91a774f67/chaos_cup_3840x2160.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.23151041666666666,yPosition=0.5712962962962963)

Alter isn’t here to play. **But we are.**

On Friday 7th June things are stirring up at New Meta Gaming Arena in London with the Apex Legends™ **Chaos Cup, the first official in person** Apex Legends™ Community Event in the UK. Alongside eight headline content creators, join the Apex Legends community for a day of Legendary chaos with Apex Legends™ to celebrate the arrival of all things Upheaval including Solos, our latest Legend Alter, and more. 

But that’s not all!

The Chaos Cup will also be streamed live 5-8pm BST Friday 7th June on the PlayApex Twitch channel. Players who register below to take part in the Team Deathmatch showdown portion of the day will have the opportunity to team up with content creators like BlackBoye, Caedrel, Melanin Gamers, Nati, Sweet Anita, The Gaming Merchant, ThatPunchkid and Simply Ashton, and compete for glory… with a few little surprises thrown in along the way. Every entrant will receive Apex Coins, but depending on how high your team places, you could end up walking away with a Razer BlackShark V2 Pro headset or even a PlayStation®5 Console or Xbox Series X.

Once the Chaos Cup winners have been crowned, embrace disorder and join us for the afterparty where you can win prizes and hang with the creators.
