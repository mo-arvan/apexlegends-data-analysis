## Supersonic Event**{#supersonic-event}**

[![Apex Legends: Supersonic Event Gameplay Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ve1VGTY0Oko)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/5jTEGUEsOumWuefgZEQ7xn/b30da69e8b8dc48680166c839fee7f83/ApexS27_Supersonic_3840x2160.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.10755208333333334,yPosition=0.1736111111111111)(Youtube)(Thumbnail for the Supersonic event YouTube trailer. )

Break the sound barrier in the Supersonic Event! Chase adrenaline on an updated Olympus Wildcard map, feel the afterburn with a missile-launching Mythic R-99, and outrun your opponents with new speed-boosting Wild Cards. 

The Supersonic Event runs from November 18 to December 16.
## WILDCARD: OLYMPUS**{#wildcard}**

![Overview shot of the Remixed Olympus map for the Wildcard mode.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/7koYMoDGNU5qmuHierbNqk/3bb750eca5937c055771e29496ab05a1/Apex_Modes-Olympus-Map_16x9.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)

Reach peak performance on Olympus. The Wildcard version of Olympus is packed with extra map toys, like ziplines and gravity cannons. Take flight and win fights, Legends!
## NEW MYTHIC R-99: BIRD OF PREY

![1st person in-game screenshot of the new R-99 skin. A legend is shooting it at another legend running. ](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/73rygbxnYcpedLMqwgjwS7/ad71998ebb9a153a5d51853706a32e50/APEX_Legends_S27_Screenshots_Supersonic_R99CUinspect1PB_v05.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)

Introducing the Bird of Prey: the new Mythic R-99. This lethal weapon is inspired by Valkyrie and master crafted in Rampart’s workshop.

In Wildcard, the Bird of Prey evolves into a missile-launching beast. Just hit your target repeatedly to activate its Missile Volley ability, which automatically fires missiles that track your target. It also boosts your walk, crouch, and sprint speed to keep you on the hunt. 

The Bird of Prey can be found through Rampart Care Package drops. Care Packages drop at the start of each round: they’ll contain one Bird of Prey and 2 random weapons, with higher rarity as the rounds progress.

## EVENT LOOT

We’re also making the following changes to the loot pool during the Supersonic Event:

- **Alternator SMG:** The Alternator hits Wildcard with the lethal addition of the “Double Tap” Hop Up and the return of Disruptor Rounds.
- **Bocek Compound Bow:** The Bocek is in rotation, but with an explosive twist. Explosive Bolts are now always active, no Frag Grenades required.
- **Pulse Blade Throwing Knife:** The Pulse Blade is back after a long hiatus. This knife detects nearby enemies with a brief sonar pulse when thrown, and an increased throwing speed means it moves even faster through the air.

## REWARD SHOP**{#reward-shop}**

Earn Boost throughout the event to get items from the Reward Shop. Earn up to 1750 Boost per day by completing daily event challenges, then check out the Supersonic Event Reward Shop to redeem them for rewards, including 2 Supersonic Event Packs.
