# The Hunt is on in the Inner Beast Collection Event


[![Apex Legends: Inner Beast Collection Event Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JtyJA36LzTw)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/7yoa8xLlBDGmKX6Isegmpa/b37311586c4527f815f61b379639f7ab/inner-beasts-ce-trailer-thumbnail.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)(Youtube)(inner-beasts-ce-trailer-thumbnail)

With the Inner Beast Collection Event, it’s kill or be killed! Track your Prey Squad using a Hunt datapad and eliminate them for a reward in the Battle Royale takeover: The Hunt. But be warned, Prey can also outlast the duration of the hunt or eliminate their Hunters to claim rewards. Ensnare limited-time event cosmetics like Bloodhound’s “Prowler Guardian” skin and the “Mawful” C.A.R. SMG skin, tailor-made for close-quarter takedowns. Capture all 24 limited-time items before the event ends to receive Octane’s Butterfly Knife heirloom—sharp enough to slice through the densest jungle...and enemy squads.
## NEW TAKEOVER: THE HUNT

![Several Apex Legends characters in purple and red armor ambush a squad in a jungle setting, while a skull icon flashes on a handheld device in the foreground.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/6rHdrMTEsewuasplW4JxjO/c833789dfb59aed08cf34a7664091584/Copy_of_S20_E02_InnerBeast_LobaHuntPad_A1_v01.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.22291666666666668,yPosition=0.4236111111111111)

Look for Hunts, unique datapads with tracking data, in non-Ranked Battle Royale matches to discover and be assigned a Prey squad for you to hunt to their elimination—or yours. Hunts are available across the map and can be activated at any time during the match. Initiate a Hunt and let the tracking begin!

Enemy squads will be randomly selected and marked as Prey for a limited-time. Keep a watchful eye as Hunters will receive updated tracking data while they’re on the prowl. And tread lightly, your Prey will be warned once you’re within a dangerous range of them (~70m). Each time a Hunt is activated, another squad is selected to be the Hunters and a second one becomes the Prey. Succeed in your tracking for a bonus reward, but if you fall to your Prey then the reward will be bestowed upon them instead. Don’t stay still for long, your squad could be next.
## COLLECTION EVENT ITEMS

Harness 24 limited-time cosmetics, with Legendary skins for Bloodhound, Octane, Caustic, and more!

All 24 items will be available in exchange for Apex Coins or Crafting Metals and in Inner Beast Event Apex Packs† for the entire duration of the event. 

If you capture them all before the event ends, you’ll automatically receive Octane’s Butterfly Knife heirloom: “Octane’s Prototype.” 
## REWARD TRACKER

Take on challenges to honor the Allfather, and unlock rewards and badges. Up to 1,400 points can be earned per day and challenges refresh daily. All of these challenges also stack with your Battle Pass so you can complete multiple challenges at once.
