# Go Full Contact in the Fight Force Event


[![Apex Legends: Fight Force Event Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rBm1ry-rUOQ)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/v2pZ2X5vY5XGIYMCmFV4o/d222eab1064ff205e8b95f2ac42ef76c/Apex_Fight_Force_Event_Trailer_Thumbnail_3840x2160.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.72578125,yPosition=0.49444444444444446)(Youtube)(Cover image for the Fight Force event Youtube video.)

Ready for a fire fight, Legends? From April 15-29, 2025, get ready to be blown away in the Fight Force Event. And by blown away, we mean the unreal, third-person view EPG Extreme LTM where every player enters with an EPG-1 Grenade Launcher and a Power Sword and that’s it. Complete Kaminari Qpi’s Creator Commissioner challenges to earn Apex Packs and a Legendary weapon skin. Progress through the Fight Force event to earn the ultimate prize: Mirage’s “Apex Adonis.” He named this Prestige skin himself, right?
## CREATOR COMMISSIONERS

Our final Creator Commissioner is Kaminari Qpi. Take on her challenges from April 15-29 with the chance to unlock the “Tombstick” Legendary Mastiff skin. In week one, the community’s time in the Outlands will determine how many Apex Packs† will be up for grabs the following week. Remember, participating in week one doesn’t automatically earn you rewards. Plus, earn a Banner Frame when you tune in to your faves on Twitch via Twitch Drops and a free04 Creator-themed tracker set in the Featured store to gift your friends!

If you haven’t already, be sure to connect your EA account with your Twitch account in order to receive drops: <u>[instructions can be found here](https://help.ea.com/en/help/account/link-your-ea-account-to-twitch/)</u>. And once your accounts are all connected, don’t miss the Support-a-Streamer campaign from April 22-May 6, 2025. Tune in to participating streamers and show your support to earn the “Cool Vibes” Rampart Skin and the “Suited Up” gun charm.
## LTM: EPG EXTREME

This LTM couldn’t have a better name: it’s extreme, alright. Armed with nothing more than a Power Sword and an EPG-1, the power is in your hands (and the hands of your enemies) in this unique third-person mode. There’s no other weapons, no other ordnance, and no other survival items here, but that’s no problem for a Legend like you right? Bring on the boom!

## REWARD TRACK

You can earn up to 1,400 points per day and challenges refresh daily. Each and every one of these challenges also stack with your Battle Pass, so you can make the most of your time in the Outlands.

## PATCH NOTES 
### MODES
###### Standard Trios (April 15 - May 5, 2025)
###### LTM: EPG Extreme (April 15-April 29, 2025)

- In collaboration with Creator Commissioner: Kaminari Qpi
- Drop in with an Extreme Loadout with EPG-1 and Power Sword, Purple Armor, and All Legend Upgrades
- 3rd person Straight Shot - Revival style with fewer squads and faster ring
- 18 Player matches, 6 teams of 3 players each
- No Loot or Loot Bins, No Inventory, No Evo Harvesters or Replicators
- Revive and Respawn faster, and Respawn with Extreme Loadout again
- Drop Armor Core on Death
- Auto Health Regen

###### LTM: Straight Shot Revival Quads (April 29-May 5, 2025)

The return of Straight Shot Revivals! This time there’s four players per squad, so bring an extra friend for even more chaos!

- 32 Player matches, 8 teams of 4 players each. 
- Straight-Shot Rules: Battle in smaller and faster rings with simplified loot. Land in pre-determined starting areas near another squad.
- Revivals: 

    - Standard Revival Rules
    - If a teammate is alive you are able to respawn once the respawn timer hits zero. 
    - Revivals turn off once your team survives to the last ring.

- Arsenals and Kitted Weapons:

    - Spawn white kitted weapons on the racks.
    - Arsenals will upgrade the weapon by 1 tier until purple. Gold upgrade is available once loot bin reset has triggered.
    - If a higher tier scope is equipped on a lower tiered weapon, the upgrade will preserve the custom scope selection.
    - If no scope is equipped, the upgrade will grant the scope of the next upgrade.
