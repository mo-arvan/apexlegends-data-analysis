# Apex Legends™: Creator Hub


The Global Creator community has been a vital part of Apex Legends since day one. 

Regardless of your size, your style or your content genre, we want to empower the content creator journey and support our multifaceted, diverse communities. In order to supercharge our creator communities, we are committing to increased communication and transparency and expanding our support for the Apex Legends creator experience.

To the content creators who have watched the Apex Legends franchise from a distance, unsure on where to begin and who to ask for help, there will be creator-focused communications on our official channels on what to expect so you can confidently take that leap.

To the creators who visit Apex Legends a few times a year, we are investing in more resources to help you make your content more engaging for your community. 

To creators who’ve yet to join the Apex Legends Universe, we’re not asking you to have a certain minimum number of subscribers, followers, or viewers - we’re seeking folks who want to make great content and build great communities.

To our core creators who have been creating Apex Legends content since launch, thank you - and do keep your eye on this space as we roll out more opportunities to go deeper with the game. 

To all creators: If you are dedicated to making great content and building amazing communities, let’s talk.

<u>Resources Open to Creators: </u>

Starting November 8 these are the resources for creators across North America, Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, Germany, and France:

- Dedicated Respawn / Apex Legends Creator Discord for creator-centric announcements, resources and updates 
- More opportunities in the [EA Creator Network™](https://creatornetwork.ea.com/interested-creators/start)
- Ready-to-use stream overlays and content creation [assets](https://www.ea.com/games/apex-legends/creator-resources)
- Direct lines of communication to request resources for creator-driven community groups and events
- Twitch Club and Guild support for extended community events 
- Creator spotlights and video compilations
- Increase in Global Twitch Drops available to creators 
- Q&amp;A sessions with our teams to provide thoughts and insights 
- At-home and in-studio playtests


**Things we’re working on:**

- Expansion of these resources to Central and South America.
- More exciting details to come for Asia Pacific
- Expansion of Vtuber models and resources at no cost
- More in-person events and meet-ups


**More to Come:** Our promise to increase communication starts now. For all creators large and small with vision, drive and an interest to work more closely with our teams, find out more [here](https://apexlegendscreatorhub.com/). We will be on the lookout for your feedback to help us continually improve and refine the support we offer.
