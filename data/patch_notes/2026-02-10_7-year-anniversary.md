## COMMUNITY-MADE REWARDS TRACKER**{#**reward-tracker**}**

Complete daily and weekly challenges to earn points in the Community-Made Reward Tracker and unlock Weapon Skins, Legend skins, Banner Frames, and Stat Trackers made for the Community by the Community.

Thank you to all of the incredibly talented and hardworking artists and creators who teamed up to bring this Reward Tracker to life!

## UNLOCK LEGENDS PERMANENTLY**{#**unlock-legends**}**

Ready to expand your roster? You’ve got 4 weeks to unlock 4 Legends. 

Each week during the 7th Anniversary drops a new set of challenges. Complete all 4 and permanently unlock that week’s Legend. Each challenge will also reward 5 Battle Pass stars.

## MILESTONE EVENT ITEMS**{#**milestone-event-items**}**

[![7yr Anniversary_Legend Skins_Blog_v1](https://drop-assets.ea.com/videos/aacW42dBFI3Fc8w7mZlRb/8456b670f651c168aadffc99437ee52a/7yr_Anniversary_Legend_Skins_Blog_v1.mp4)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/1Ah0XtEyMkCCxmaPJbO30V/6f633de299cfd59d2400953f1a03cd8b/legend-skins-7yr-anniversary-apex.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.4405940594059406)(Mp4)(Thumbnail image for the legend skins for Apex's 7th Anniversary event.)

Check out the new tactical Legendary Skins! We’ve got fresh fits for Alter, Wraith, Newcastle, Sparrow, Fuse and Rampart. 

[![7yr Anniversary_Weapon Skins_Blog_v1](https://drop-assets.ea.com/videos/1CxxGkbQ4a1KohgyJeBt8Z/6eb080cf892da104204585c7d8527baa/7yr_Anniversary_Weapon_Skins_Blog_v1.mp4)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/7wSPDkjaGoV2Cyb7NfbBE6/6c39504b219ab355fca1f8f4cd83b024/weapon-skins-7yr-anniversary-apex.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.45703125,yPosition=0.4801980198019802)(Mp4)(Thumbnail image for the weapon skins for Apex's 7th Anniversary event.)

And new Legendary Weapon Skins for the Mozambique, R-301, Alternator, RE-45, Wingman, and Flatline. 

All 36 items are available as possible drops in 7th Anniversary Event Packs†. Each pack contains one event item and two standard items, and there are no duplicates of event items in these packs. 

[![7yr Anniversary_Mythic Peacekeeper_Blog_v1](https://drop-assets.ea.com/videos/5KnwAB8WGcTzQ6g3o7CemX/9ab7337d97fce9098658d86ae759e7fa/7yr_Anniversary_Mythic_Peacekeeper_Blog_v1.mp4)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/1plxTuAjFK8w6foorjLGWa/3c1b3656fdceb46265be83079c53412c/pk-heirloom-skin-7yr-anniversary-apex.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.37994791666666666,yPosition=0.4803611738148984)(Mp4)(Thumbnail image for the weapon skins for Apex's 7th Anniversary event.)

Time to make waves, bruddah. Introducing the Riptide Predator: the new Mythic Peacekeeper, combining the raw power of the sea with the unstoppable force of Gibraltar himself. 

Use Exotic Shards to unlock 3 additional skin variants: Bone Warden, Violet Undertow, and Blood in the Water.

![Screenshot of all the rewards and the milestone trackers for rewards for the 7th Anniversary event.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/56IPasqk3uo56DZNe419rH/e6c028267fafb9371ad4aab25e0f608f/7yr_Anniversary_Milestone_Progression.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.24583333333333332,yPosition=0.6673267326732674)

Unlock a Reactive Weapon Pack as you progress through the Milestone Reward Track, guaranteed to contain a Reactive Weapon you don’t already own. Already collected all the Reactive Weapon Skins? No problem, you’ll get 50 Exotic Shards instead.
