# Get your head in the Galactic Games Event


[![Apex Legends: Galactic Games Event](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmI5vT8DvsY)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/6qldWDGhKlyRKH1Rres4k1/6770cfc1efa5a6bf537ab253b606b6fd/APEX_S26_GGE_3840x2160.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.20598958333333334,yPosition=0.3143518518518518)(Youtube)(Thumbnail for the YouTube trailer for the Galactic Games milestone event.)

Welcome to the Galactic Games, Legends! Consider this your invitation to three weeks of wild times in the Outlands. Wildcard has been updated with a new, aptly named, Wild Card mechanic—giving you a chance to change the tides with new craftables and limited-time variants for the event. Test your grip and grit with the Grand Slam Bat, available in Wildcard Care Packages during the event. You’ll have a chance to unlock a universal melee variant of the Bat alongside all-star skins for fan favorite Legends and Legendary skins for the R-301, Flatline, and more as you progress through the event. It’s yeet or be yeeted from August 19 to September 9, 2025. 
## GALACTIC GAMES IN WILDCARD 

![Loba takes a swing with the Grand Slam Bat.](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/3q3EnVzGNPtcfBuY1sqrTQ/2ac2c2cc471f5976ee4d4b73e600603e/Copy_of_APEX_Legends_s26_Screenshots_GalacticGames_RD3_LobaBat_B_v01_CLEAN.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.29322916666666665,yPosition=0.293287037037037)

Get ready to knock Legends out of the ring with the Grand Slam Bat—available for everyone for a limited time in Wildcard, no unlocking required. This ultimate choice for fans of hefty melee damage also deals knock backs and an even bigger launch after the third successful hit with the potential to impact multiple enemies. Available in Wildcard Care Packages for the duration of the event.

Replicators have been refreshed with a new craftable to help take the edge off: Wild Cards. Craft and activate these little beauties for access to powerful abilities like wall-running, extra melee knockback, or bonus melee damage. You’ll unlock a new slot at the start of Rounds 1, 2, and 3, allowing you to equip up to three Wild Cards of your choosing to help you reach the podium. Here’s our Wild Card team stepping up to the plate for this limited-time event:

- Mad Dash: gain a dash ability
- Recharger: recharge shields while running
- Personal Space: melee knockback is increased
- Hard Hitter: melee damage is increased
- Status Report: reveal enemy locations on knock
- Wall Runner: gain the ability to Wall-Run
- Speedy Charge: Tactical and Ultimate abilities charge faster


Much like Wildcard itself, this new mechanic will be sticking around and receiving updates as we continue to play in this new ground. Step up to bat while you can, Wildcard returns to its core experience September 9-15, 2025.
## REWARD SHOP

Earn Honor by completing daily event challenges, then check out the Galactic Games Reward Shop to redeem for rewards and check your progress with all four event badges. Offerings will rotate halfway through the event.

## EVENT ITEMS 

[![galactic-games-me-legend-skins](https://drop-assets.ea.com/videos/6UV9xlEvWjA7rbu0e0c9Tp/bd1fd84e169767a75d6a1b0e57c74245/Copy_of_Galactic_Games_Legend_Skins_Blog_v1.mp4)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/4Xu7AXHIcvJWvgnjdyKZCt/60ad702a2162b9558ef50d4448b12bd7/Screenshot_2025-08-08_093218.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.2071389940508383,yPosition=0.35721153846153847)(Mp4)(Thumbnail image for the video showcasing the new legend skins in the Galactic Games milestone event.)

Godlike limited-time cosmetics are up for grabs as you progress through the Galactic Games Event, including Legendary skins for Seer, Sparrow, Gibraltar, and more. Each item received counts towards bonus rewards that are automatically unlocked at milestones along the way including Exotic Shards.  

[![galactic-games-me-weapon-skins](https://drop-assets.ea.com/videos/2tE26d0MPwqldQtInns3JT/6a77bdac06778acc652745b88535f8cd/Copy_of_Galactic_Games_Weapon_Skins_Blog_v1.mp4)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/3DKSH25mNynSVwDpk0wtK2/c82b8b370c8909f116f60cddeff4e2d2/Screenshot_2025-08-08_093338.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.3052317701274058,yPosition=0.3053012048192771)(Mp4)(Thumbnail image for the video showcasing the new weapon skins in the Galactic Games milestone event.)

All items will be available as possible drops in Galactic Games Event Packs† for the duration of the event, with each pack having a chance to unlock the new universal melee, Grand Slam Bat. Each event pack contains one event item and two standard items, and there are no duplicates of event items in these packs. 

[![galactic-games-me-grand-slam-bat](https://drop-assets.ea.com/videos/4ssBapiJW62OWpRAETkaII/181b10a97f2c7fefa4a291c30e7651ec/Copy_of_Myhtic_Bat_Blog_v1.mp4)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/7wr77Ihv79JmpGCISsmacP/6ecf46283aa71aa95569293fd50e2a23/Screenshot_2025-08-08_093012.png?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.3067284118768728,yPosition=0.30677966101694915)(Mp4)(Thumbnail image for the video showcasing the new Grand Slam Bat in the Galactic Games milestone event.)

The Grand Slam Bat that you’ll add to your collection functions similarly to other universal melee items, so it won’t be quite as hefty as its Wildcard variant, but it does have a bit of visual fire when it’s equipped. Customizations for this new universal melee will be available for Exotic Shards with three different skins and the matching Death Box.
