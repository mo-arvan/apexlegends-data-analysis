# Twilight Befalls E-District in the Techno Terror Collection Event


[![Apex Legends: Techno Terror Collection Event Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vNkvnDqb24Y)](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/4yWTXsG57efic8HG4q4zvc/b1ff34a96e83ac4b4044c57c098c7894/apex-techno-terror.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)(Youtube)(Apex Legends: Shockwave Techno Terror Collection Event. Features futuristic warriors and vibrant neon colors. )

Haunting time is upon us, Legends! Trick N’ Treat Trios returns along with some familiar tricks and treats: Copycat Kits giving you the power of other Legends, Rev Shell Grenades to hunt enemies with wicked precision, and munch on candy for shields, EVO, and Ultimate charge. Complete a new Reward Track for Epic Skins for Mirage and the Devotion. Twilight has befallen the streets of E-District and the time to terrorize your enemies is now, but we’ve still got one more treat up our sleeve. Complete your killer 24 event item collection before the event ends to summon Pathfinder’s Prestige Skin: “Apex Mecha.” Don’t miss out on the terror—it’s time to scare the competition, to death!
### TRICK N’ TREAT TRIOS RETURNS

![Trick or Treat Trios](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/6eYqq6R3vR3JRVNa1rmZ1Z/ae563ff93995fcba11fff7f0298df33d/trick-and-treat-trios.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)

The hair raising classic is back with the revival twist! Knock on doors to collect candy? Nah, knock your enemies, break the doors, and loot all the Loot Bins to collect and consume sweets for your extra treat: EVO points, Ultimate charge, and 25 points of shield healing over time. Tricks aren’t just for kids. Activate a Copycat Kit to gain access to an extra ability from a different Legend for the rest of the match or take out the enemy with updated Rev Shells that look deadlier than ever. And with the addition of the revival mechanic, it ain’t over until it’s over. Get back in the fight and show them who the real terror is. 
### E-DISTRICT AT NIGHT

![E-District and Night](https://drop-assets.ea.com/images/1NJAqt11shpWkEbyi22Emw/110e0ad219b3471ba40f20c826631c4e/e-district-at-night-v1.jpg?im=AspectCrop=%2816,9%29,xPosition=0.5,yPosition=0.5)

E-District, a once-thriving urban island in the heart of Suotamo on Gaea, now twisted by a terrifying cybernetic infestation. This island with its weathered blackouts, riots, and a chemical attack at Humbert Labs, is no longer a mere cityscape—it’s a corrupted, electrified labyrinth of horror, brought back to life by an ominous power.

Battle your way through the decaying heart of the Energy Bank, where towering high-rise is now overrun by cybernetic abominations. Engage in brutal combat within the Stadium, a once-vibrant arena now transformed into an infected city of conflict, its streets and alleys infested with digital horrors. New dangers hide around every corner and every encounter will test your resolve. Explore, survive, and uncover the secrets of this corrupted city, where the line between legend and reality has been obliterated.
### COLLECTION EVENT ITEMS

Unlock 24 killer limited-time cosmetics, with Legendary skins for Alter, Wattson, and more!

All 24 items will be available for direct unlock with Apex Coins or Crafting Metals, or test your luck with Techno Terror Event Apex Packs† for the entire duration of the event.

Grab them all before Techno Terror ends and you’ll automatically receive Pathfinder’s Prestige Skin, “Apex Mecha,” which comes with the “A Blast from the Path” Finisher, and the “Block Party” Skydive Trail.
### REWARD TRACKER

You can earn up to 1,400 points per day and challenges refresh daily. All these challenges also stack with your Battle Pass so you can complete multiple challenges at once.

### STORE TAB

Explore limited-time offers in the Store tab including the Weekend Killer bundle and the Cyborg Warlord Bundle available beginning October 15th.

FIGHT OR FRIGHT SALE

In addition to the limited-time offers mentioned above, the Fight or Fright sale will be a treat when it hits the shop! From October 1st - November 5th, enjoy limited-time discounts on returning eerie skins. No tricks here—there will be two new Legendary skins for you to discover: the “Reaper’s Path” skin for Pathfinder and the “Lionhearted” Conduit skin.
