The '''Rampage LMG''', or '''Rampage''', is a fully-automatic [[light machine gun]] introduced in {{ItemLink|Season 3 (Mobile)|text=Season 3}} that utilizes {{ItemLink|Heavy Rounds}}.

The Rampage has a unique [[File:Heavy Rounds Revved Up.svg|20px|Revved Up]] Revved Up mechanic that consumes a {{ItemLink|Thermite Grenade}}. It takes 4 seconds to consume the Thermite, increasing the fire rate of the Rampage by 30% for 90 seconds, even though each shot decreases the remaining charge time by 2 seconds. In this state, the Rampage can also break doors in two shots, or one if the door was already damaged.
