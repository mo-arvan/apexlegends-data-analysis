Grenades are throwable explosive [[weapons]] that go in the inventory like items. There are currently three types of grenades: the Frag Grenade, the Thermite Grenade, and the Arc Star.

Grenades can be accessed by holding {{Key|G}} or {{Xbox|Right}}/{{PS4|Right}}. You can then use the fire button on PC, {{Xbox|RT}} for Xbox or {{PS4|R2}} for the PS5 to aim and throw.

== Thermite Grenade ==
{{Items
| name = Thermite Grenade
| image = Thermite Grenade.svg
| type = Thrown
| Time to use = Instant
| description = Creates a horizontal wall of flames.
| info = 
* The wall is 6 m long and is horizontal, perpendicular to the direction it was thrown from.
* Deals 4 damage per tick when standing in the flames, and applies an over-time burn effect that lasts for 25 damage.
* Bypasses the {{ItemLink|Knockdown Shield}}.
* Damages and destroys doors.
* The wall of flames lasts for 8 seconds after impact with the ground.
* Can be used to increase the fire rate of the [[Rampage LMG]].
| tips = 
* It can be useful to seal off an enclosed area such as a doorway to prevent enemies from pushing a position, as well as a mild way to break the line of sight of an enemy.
* If an enemy is body blocking a door, you can throw a Thermite at the bottom of the door. It will destroy the door and burn the enemy.
* Can be useful against an enemy raising their {{ItemLink|Knockdown Shield}}, as raising the shield slows them and forces them to take more damage from the flames.

== Arc Star ==
{{Items
| name = Arc Star
| image = Arc Star.svg
| type = Thrown
| Time to use = 2.8 seconds
| description = Sticks, then explodes after a short delay. Causes damage and blurred vision.
| info = 
* Deals 10 impact damage and up to 75 explosion damage in an 8.75 m explosion radius, with a 1.8 m inner radius. Slows those affected for up to 5 seconds.
* Projectile launch speed is faster than the other grenades (2200 UPS instead of 1300 UPS).
* Damages and destroys doors. Also, it disables the [[Trident]] for a very short amount of time.
* Does not stick to squadmates, will instead bounce off them.
| tips = 
* The explosion's "splash" damage decreases with distance from the Arc Star itself so plan accordingly.
* If you come across extra high-tier armor such as {{Epic}} or {{Legendary}} ones and you or your squad don't need it, equip an Arc Star, look at your feet and throw the Arc Star on the ground. You'll break your armor, but now you can swap to the new extra armor. Thus, leaving a broken armor on the ground for an enemy to potentially pick up or be forced to heal it.
* Some legends can shake the stuck Arc Star off by activating their ability. For example, {{AbilityLink|Into the Void}} by Wraith and  {{AbilityLink|Phase Breach}} by Ash.

== Rev Shell ==
{{Items
| name = Rev Shell
| image = Rev Shell.svg
| type = Thrown & Homing
| Time to use = On impact
| description = Automatic target seeking grenade, throw it in the direction of enemies and watch rev shell hunt down its prey.
| info = 
* Exclusive to the [[Living Shell Trios]] mode.
* Flies in a straight line until it locates a nearby enemy, after which it will lock on to them or else it vanishes after flying for a certain time.
** Target will be marked on player's HUD.
** The targets will know when they are is locked on the Rev Shell, as a message reading "Rev Shell Lock On" and the Rev Shell indicator will appear on their HUD.
* After reaching its target, it stops and take 0.5 seconds to explode, dealing 58 ~ 79<!-- Not confirm --> damage.
* It has 30 HP. Can be shot down or melee when close enough.
| tips = 
* It can be used as probe to seek enemy in the hiding spot to avoid being ambushed.
* As it can be shot down easily, when it starts chasing the target, provide suppressing fire on the locked-on target so they could get distracted.
| interactions =
* [[Fuse]] can hold two Rev Shells per stack, but he doesn't launch them with his passive ability {{AbilityLink|Grenadier}}.
* Rev Shells cannot be used to open the [[Explosive Holds]] doors.
* Can be attached to the {{AbilityLink|Nox Gas Trap}}, {{AbilityLink|Riot Drill}}, {{AbilityLink|Launch Pad}}, {{AbilityLink|Gravity Lift}}, {{AbilityLink|Black Hole}}, {{AbilityLink|Exhibit}} and {{AbilityLink|Piercing Spikes}}.
* Can be grappled with the {{AbilityLink|Grappling Hook}}.
