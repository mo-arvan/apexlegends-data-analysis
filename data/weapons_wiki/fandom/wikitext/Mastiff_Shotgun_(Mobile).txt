The '''Mastiff Shotgun''', or '''Mastiff''', is a semi-automatic [[shotgun]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Shotgun Shells}}. It fires 8 pellets in the shape of a horizontal line, which decreases in width while aiming down the sights, reducing the spread and thus increasing accuracy.

== Pros and cons ==
; Pros
*High damage per shot.
*Players without a {{Mythic|Level 5}} {{ItemLink|Evo Shield}} can be downed in two shots, making the time-to-kill incredibly low.
*1× leg shot multiplier.
*Aiming down the sights reduces spread.
*Ability to reload bullets between shots.

; Cons
*Animation between shots obscures vision.
*1× head shot multiplier at 38+ meters.
*Hipfire has increased spread.
**This increased spread can however increase the likelihood of shots connecting.
*Slowest empty reload of all shotguns due to each shell being loaded individually.

== Lore ==
The M1901 Mastiff Combat Shotgun was developed specifically for low-gravity close-quarters engagements. The kinetic energy produced when fired in low-gravity can severely unbalance its user with massive recoil, so the engineers at [[Lastimosa Armory]] incorporated a muzzle velocity dampening system which mitigates that energy and greatly reduces recoil.

Intended for low to zero gravity ship boarding and clearing operations, the Mastiff gained notoriety throughout [[The Frontier]] as a reliable and devastating combat shotgun. Shells can be fed through the under-mounted magazine or with the optional drum magazine upgrade. Troops from the [[IMC | IMC's]] 401st often refer to it as the "Mobile Claymore" as its high rate of fire proved to be devastating.<ref name="ArtStation">[https://www.artstation.com/artwork/l5zGV Ryan Lastimosa on ArtStation]</ref>
