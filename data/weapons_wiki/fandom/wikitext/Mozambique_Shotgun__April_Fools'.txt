The '''April Fools'''' variant of the [[Mozambique Shotgun]] is a fully automatic triple-barrel [[shotgun pistol]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Shotgun Shells}}. It shoots three pellets in the shape of a triangle.

When enabled, this version of the Mozambique replaces the standard version.

Being a shotgun, the April Fools' Mozambique affects [[Mad Maggie]]'s movement speed because of her {{AbilityLink|Warlord's Ire}} Passive ability.

This is one of the seven April Fools' weapons in ''Apex Legends'', the other six being: [[P2020/April Fools'|April Fools' P2020]], [[Mozambeam]], [[Mozamblam]], [[Mozambrrr]], [[Mozamburst]], and [[Ol' Nessie]].
