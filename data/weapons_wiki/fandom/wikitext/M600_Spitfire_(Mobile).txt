== Pros and Cons ==
'''Pros'''

* High damage, especially when aiming for the head.
* Only three attachments slots, making fully kitting out fairly easy.
* Large magazine size.

'''Cons'''

* Moderate recoil, and no Barrel mod can be accepted.
* Lower fire-rate than other LMGs.
** Slower TTK compared to other LMGs.
* Higher bullet drop than other LMGs.
