The '''G7 Scout''' is a [[marksman rifle]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Light Rounds}}. It fires semi-automatically or, by equipping a {{ItemLink|Double Tap Trigger|text={{Epic|Double Tap Trigger}}}} Hop-Up, it may also be switched to a 2-round burst fire mode.

== Trivia ==
* The G7 appearance is based on the real [[wikipedia:Springfield Armory M1A#SOCOM rifles|Springfield Armory M1A SOCOM 16]] semi-automatic rifle fitted with a [http://www.jallenglobal.com/jae-100-g3-standard-orders/ J. Allen Enterprises JAE-100 chassis].
* The G7 is [[Bangalore]]'s favorite weapon. It is seen in a few of her banner poses, her character select animation, and her lobby animations.
