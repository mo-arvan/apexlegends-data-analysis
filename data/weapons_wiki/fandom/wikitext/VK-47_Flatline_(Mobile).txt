== Pros And Cons ==
'''Pros'''

* Excellent damage for a fully-automatic weapon.
* Reasonably high fire-rate for a Heavy Ammo weapon.
* Three attachment slots, making fully kitting convenient.
* Larger magazine than other rifles, excluding the Havoc, at 22 rounds by default.
*Ability to swap between Automatic and Semi-Auto.

'''Cons'''

* Moderate horizontal recoil and cannot equip a Barrel mod.
* Lower fire-rate than other rifles.
*Moderate reload time, on-par with the Hemlok, faster than the Havoc, but slower than the R-301 Carbine.
*Recoil is still present in Semi-Auto.
