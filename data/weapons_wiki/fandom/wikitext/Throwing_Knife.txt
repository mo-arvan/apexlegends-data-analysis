The '''Throwing Knife''' is a throwable weapon introduced in {{ItemLink|Season 14}} that utilizes its own ammo type, exclusive to {{ItemLink|Gun Run}} and the [[Firing Range]].

It automatically replenishes itself after getting thrown, essentially having unlimited ammo.
