The '''Sentinel''' is a bolt-action [[sniper rifle]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Sniper Ammo}}.

It has a unique [[File:Sniper Ammo Amped.png|25px]] '''Disruptor''' mechanic: two (one with a {{ItemLink|Body Shield|text={{Legendary|Body Shield}}}}) {{ItemLink|Shield Cell|text=Shield Cells}} charge up the Sentinel, making it shots guaranteed to shatter enemy's [[Shield]] for 120 seconds, and each shot will shorten the charge by 15 seconds. Charging the Sentinel takes 5 seconds.

== Lore ==
The Sentinel ESR ('''E'''lectromagnetic '''S'''niper '''R'''ifle) is a dual-stage, electromagnetic actuated sniper rifle manufactured by [[Paradinha Arsenal]]. Rounds are chambered through the rear bolt action receiver and fired with a traditional closed bolt pin. The round is then accelerated with a muzzle velocity of 5km/s through the EM Propulsion system. This delivers a projectile with highly destructive kinetic energy to defeat the immediate effects of bullet drop over distances of 2km. This gives the weapon more accessibility and a faster cycle time than the [[Kraber .50-Cal Sniper]] in exchange for a little less lethality.<ref>[https://www.artstation.com/artwork/EV6NAn Ryan Lastimosa on ArtStation]</ref>
