The '''Peacekeeper''' is a lever-action energy [[shotgun]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Shotgun Shells}}. It fires 9 pellets in the shape of a shield, with one pellet in the middle and two on each outer line of the star shape. When {{ItemLink|Choke Active Icon|noText=1}} Choke is active, the spread of the pellets is greatly reduced while aiming down sights.

The Peacekeeper can be enhanced with {{ItemLink|Disruptor Rounds|text={{Legendary|Disruptor Rounds}}}}, which increases damage dealt against shielded targets by 25%.

Being a shotgun, the Peacekeeper affects [[Mad Maggie]]'s movement speed because of her {{AbilityLink|Warlord's Ire}} Passive ability.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|As manufacturing became more commonplace with IMC expansion, the need to guard IMC factories and plants against outlaws increased. However, many ballistic firearms could not be used in these facilities due to the volatile materials involved in industrial production. The IMC turned to Lastimosa Armory for a solution, and the energy-based Peacekeeper was born. Slow and heavy-hitting, the Peacekeeper requires few charges to put down a target at close range.}}

== Trivia ==
* The binary code on the {{Legendary|Pal 9000}} skin reads "Kill [[Pathfinder]]".
* The Peacekeeper's codename is "energy_shotgun", and it fires blue projectiles like energy weapons. This is due to the Peacekeeper's ammunition actually being batteries.<ref name="Lastimosa">[https://www.artstation.com/artwork/rRZEXE Ryan Lastimosa on ArtStation]</ref> Despite this, it's doesn't utilize {{ItemLink|Energy Ammo}} most likely for gameplay reasons.
* [[Gibraltar]] has a "spinning" draw pickup Peackeeper animation, similar to reloading or chambering the next shot with a single hand. This same animation is seen when using a zip line or any other action that may use a Legend's left arm.
** The spinning draw animation is also a possible reference to the 1991 science fiction film ''[[wikipedia:Terminator_2:_Judgment_Day|Terminator 2: Judgement Day]]''.
** Gibraltar and [[Fuse]] have chambering the next shot with a single hand animation in 3rd Person View.
