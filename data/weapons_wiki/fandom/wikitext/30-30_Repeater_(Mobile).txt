The '''30-30''' is a [[lever action repeating rifle]] introduced in {{ItemLink|Season 1 (Mobile)|text=Season 1}} that utilizes {{ItemLink|Heavy Rounds}}. It has a unique charge mechanic allowing the weapon to do more damage (up to 36% more) by aiming down sights for longer without firing. It can also be simply fired without charging at a faster fire rate.

== Trivia ==
* The name is a reference to the [[wikipedia:.30-30 Winchester|.30-30 Winchester]] cartridge used by the [[wikipedia:Winchester Model 1894|Winchester Model 1894]] rifle, on which the weapon's design is based.
* The 30-30 is one of the only two weapons in the game to reload one bullet at a time, the other one being the [[Mastiff Shotgun]].
