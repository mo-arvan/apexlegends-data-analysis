The '''April Fools'''' variant of the [[P2020]] is a semi-automatic [[pistol]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Light Rounds}}.

When enabled, this version of the P2020 replaces the standard version.

This is one of the seven April Fools' weapons in ''Apex Legends'', the other six being: [[Mozambique Shotgun/April Fools'|April Fools' Mozambique Shotgun]], [[Mozambeam]], [[Mozamblam]], [[Mozambrrr]], [[Mozamburst]], and [[Ol' Nessie]].
