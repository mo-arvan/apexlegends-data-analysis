'''Rampart's Custom Modded Paintball Weapons''',<ref>[https://www.ea.com/games/apex-legends/news/evolution-collection-event Evolution Collection Event Patch Notes]</ref> also referred to as '''Rampart Specials''', are variants of [[Fully Kitted Weapons]] equipped with the {{ItemLink|Splatter Rounds}} Hop-Up, which increases their reload speed by 25% and applies a purely cosmetic paintball-like effect on projectiles, varying between yellow, purple, and green randomly.

Like Fully Kitted Weapons, optics can be removed and/or switched with other compatible optics, and the other [[attachments]]' rarity match the rarity of the weapon when possible. Rampart Specials also feature the typical Fully Kitted Weapons HUD look, with a number of stars based on the rarity of the weapon, but with the addition of paint splatters. The splatters are also present when viewing a Rampart Special through the inventory.

Unlike Battle Royale's seasonal Fully Kitted Weapons, Rampart Specials are exclusive to [[Big Maude]]'s Vend-It Machines located on [[World's Edge]], require {{ItemLink|Materials}} to be obtained, and come in various rarities like {{ItemLink|Arenas}}' Fully Kitted Weapons. Also, due to already being equipped with the {{ItemLink|Splatter Rounds}} Hop-Up, other Hop-Ups that a weapon can possibly have are overridden. Prices for Rampart Specials are based on their rarity:

* {{Legendary}}: {{Materials|100}}
* {{Epic}}: {{Materials|75}}
* {{Rare}}: {{Materials|50}}.

Rampart Specials can be stolen by [[Loba]] when using a {{AbilityLink|Black Market Boutique}}. Stealing one however triggers an alarm on Big Maude and the Black Market gets destroyed, dealing 10 damage to everyone near it.

Buying or stealing a Rampart Special will trigger an announcement in the shopping area by a voice clip recorded by [[Rampart]].

Rampart Specials are not available in the Vend-it Machines during the [[Armed and Dangerous]] game mode.

== Available weapons ==
This is a list of all the possible Rampart's Custom Modded Paintball Weapons that can spawn on Big Maude. Two weapons of each rarity will spawn everytime the map is loaded, and the weapons can be duplicates ({{Texttip|e.g.|for example}} two [[R-301 Carbine|{{Legendary|R-301 Carbines}}]] may spawn). All weapons have the same chance of spawning.

Reload time is expressed in seconds.
