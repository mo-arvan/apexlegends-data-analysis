The '''Kraber''' is a bolt-action [[sniper rifle]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Mythic Sniper Ammo}}.

Being a [[Supply Drop Weapon]], it can only be found in [[Care Package]]s, and cannot be equipped with foreign ammo. It comes pre-equipped with 10 backup ammo and a [[Attachments#Optics|{{Heirloom|6x-10x Variable Sniper}}]] scope.

== Pros and Cons ==

; Pros

* Highest damage per bullet in the game.
* A headshot will always down a player instantly.
* Can cause collateral shots, as projectiles are able to pierce through multiple enemies.
* Somewhat narrow hipfire accuracy.
* Uses exclusive ammo, which does not take up inventory space.

; Cons

* Unlike the [[Sentinel (Mobile)|Sentinel]], rechambering a shot forces the player to zoom out.
* Lowest fire rate in the game.
* Very slow weapon animations. This includes drawing, scoping in, firing, rechambering a bullet, holstering and/or switching weapons, and reloading.
* Slowest projectile speed and strongest bullet drop of all sniper rifles.
* Only 10 reserve ammo (or 2 full reloads) is available per Kraber, forcing a weapon change upon depleting all of it.
* Optics cannot be changed.
