{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapons}} are {{Mythic}}-tier [[weapon]]s that can only be found in [[Care Package]]s. These weapons come with a limited amount of [[Ammo]], and most of them are equipped with their highest tier of [[Attachment]]s that cannot be removed from the weapon, along with removable [[Optics]].

==History==
{{Patch notes|
; {{Symbol|season 14}} [[August 09, 2022 Patch]]
* Removed the [[G7 Scout]] and [[Volt SMG]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[Bocek Compound Bow]] and [[Rampage LMG]].

; {{Symbol|season 13}} [[May 10, 2022 Patch]]
* Removed the [[M600 Spitfire]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[Mastiff Shotgun]].

; {{Symbol|season 12}} [[February 08, 2022 Patch]] 
* Removed the [[Alternator SMG]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[Volt SMG]].

; {{Symbol|season 11}} [[November 02, 2021 Patch]] 
* Removed the [[Triple Take]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[G7 Scout]].

; {{Symbol|season 10}} [[August 03, 2021 Patch]]
* Removed the [[Prowler Burst PDW]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[M600 Spitfire]] and [[Alternator SMG]]. 

; {{Symbol|season 9}} [[May 04, 2021 Patch]]
* Removed the [[Peacekeeper]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[Triple Take]].
* Airdrop spawn rates have changed. The early game will spawn more [[Triple Take]]s, mid-game will spawn more [[Kraber]]s, and the late game will spawn more [[Prowler]]s.

; [[March 09, 2021 Patch]]
* Optics on the [[Peacekeeper]] and [[Prowler Burst PDW]] can now be removed and replaced with a different one.

; {{Symbol|season 7}} [[November 04, 2020 Patch]]
* Removed the [[R-99 SMG]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[Prowler Burst PDW]].

; {{Symbol|season 6}} [[August 18, 2020 Patch]]
* Changed rarity of Supply Drop Weapons from {{Legendary}} to {{Mythic|Heirloom}} (now called {{Mythic}}).
* Removed the [[Devotion LMG]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[R-99 SMG]].

; {{Symbol|season 5}} [[May 12, 2020 Patch]]
* Removed the [[Mastiff Shotgun]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[Peacekeeper]].

; {{Symbol|season 4}} [[February 04, 2020 Patch]]
* Removed the [[L-STAR EMG]] from Care Packages, replaced with the [[Devotion LMG]].

; {{Symbol|season 2}} [[July 02, 2019 Patch]]
* Added the [[L-STAR EMG]] as a Supply Drop Weapon. 
* Adjusted weapon pool to vary by round; [[Kraber .50-cal Sniper]]s are more common early game while [[Mastiff Shotgun]]s are more common late game.
