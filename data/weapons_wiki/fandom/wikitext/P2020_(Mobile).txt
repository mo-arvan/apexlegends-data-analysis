== Pros and cons ==
; Pros
* Very low recoil.
* Good hipfire.
* Fast reload.
*Free in Arenas, and automatically given if the player has an open weapon slot.
**Low Material cost to upgrade in Arenas
*Excellent unshielded damage if Hammerpoint Rounds is equipped.
*Large magazine by default, at 18 rounds.
*Clean iron sights.

; Cons
* Semi-auto and requires fast tapping.
**Hipfiring while moving will cause the user to lower the weapon and raise it again as they are firing, resulting in a slightly lowered fire-rate.
* Slow projectile speed.
*Fair damage output.
**Lower TTK due to semi-auto nature and aforementioned damage.

== Lore ==
The Hammond P2020, is a semi-automatic single-action small caliber pistol manufactured by [[Lastimosa Armory]]. This handgun features an integrated standoff device and a fully milled slide. It also has a fully integrated Taclight and LAM, along with varied functions for IR, LiDAR, or FLIR illumination. When dropping in hot and you need something more than your mitts, the P2020 will help you tap, rack, and bang to first place with the best [[Legends]] out there.

== Trivia ==
* Its predecessors, the '''P2011''' and '''P2016''', were references to the year development of Titanfall 1 started and Titanfall 2's release year respectively. Following the naming scheme, the P2020 was supposed to be the P2019 but was changed before release to its current name.
