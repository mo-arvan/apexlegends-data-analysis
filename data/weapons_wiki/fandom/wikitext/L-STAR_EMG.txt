The '''L-STAR EMG''', or '''L-STAR''', is a fully-automatic [[energy machine gun]] introduced in {{ItemLink|Season 2}} that utilizes {{ItemLink|Energy Ammo}}. It fires red plasma orb-like projectiles and forcefully opens doors when it comes in contact.

The L-STAR has a unique overheating mechanic, which triggers after {{Level0123|24|26|28|30}} continuous shots; it only needs to reload if it overheats.

Being an LMG, the L-STAR is affected by [[Rampart]]'s {{AbilityLink|Modded Loader}} Passive ability.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|The L-STAR is most accurately described as an automatic superheated plasma thrower. It fires large, combustible plasma projectiles, propelled from an internal superheating chamber. A special lens focuses the projectile, and uninterrupted fire can cause this lens to overheat and shatter. "Feathering" the trigger is ideal, as it keeps the lens at a stable temperature while allowing continuous, personnel-suppressing bombardments.}}

The L-STAR EMG (Lastimosa Armory Energy Machine Gun) is a cyclic particle accelerating weaponry designed by its namesake [[Lastimosa Armory]]. With a small oscillating field generator, the L-STAR can deliver a series of charged plasma bursts with a high rate of fire. The charged particles can eat away shields and melt dense materials with sustained exposure.

Due to the high temperatures generated by the particle accelerator, sustained firing of the weapon makes it susceptible to overheating, melting the generator core.

The model used in the [[Apex Games]] is made by [[Wonyeon Interstellar]]. A few changes were made to the silhouette of this weapon to better serve the purpose of an LMG. A front bipod was attached along with slimming down the foregrip handguard to allow for firing the weapon in a rested position.<ref name="ArtStation">[https://www.artstation.com/artwork/mqwxN1 Ryan Lastimosa on ArtStation]</ref>

== Trivia ==
* Concept art of the L-STAR in ''Titanfall 2'' shows that the weapon was supposed to be a submachine gun called ASW (Advanced Scattering Weapon).
* The L-STAR appeared way before its actual release in {{ItemLink|Season 2}}, in the first ever background before logging into the lobby during the {{ItemLink|Preseason}}.
* When it was first released in Season 2, in addition to the overheat mechanics, it had a set magazine size instead of just drawing ammo from the inventory, and thus required manual reload.
** The magazine from the L-STAR's reloadable iteration is used as the model for {{ItemLink|Extended Energy Mag|text=Extended Energy Mags}}.
** When first shown in its trailer, the L-STAR was supposed to have a 60 round magazine, and it would have fired 30 shots before overheating. However, most likely due to game balance, the final release version had a magazine capacity of 40, and the gun overheated after firing 21-22 shots.
** The model of the L-STAR used in ''[[Apex Legends]]'' was made by Wonyeon Interstellar, instead of Lastimosa Armory like in ''Titanfall 2''. This might explain the initial difference in behavior between the ''[[titanfall:L-STAR|Titanfall 2]]'' and ''Apex Legends''' version of the L-STAR. With the rework of the weapon that came with {{ItemLink|Season 4}}, they now function the same.
* The L-STAR's icon lacks iron sights.
* An {{ItemLink|Extended Energy Mag|text={{Legendary|Extended Energy Mag}}}} won't autoreload the L-STAR if its cooler has overheated and requires to be changed.
