[[File:Golden R-301 Carbine.png|350px|thumb|right|A Fully Kitted {{Legendary}} [[R-301 Carbine]].]]
'''Fully Kitted Weapons''' are [[weapons]] equipped with all their possible [[attachments]] which cannot be removed, except for the [[optic]]. If the weapon is dropped, the optic still stays on the weapon. There is no performance difference between a Fully Kitted Weapon and a weapon that has been manually equipped with the same attachments.

Fully Kitted Weapons are tiered {{Common}} ({{Common|★}}), {{Rare}} ({{Rare|★★}}), {{Epic}} ({{Epic|★★★}}), and {{Legendary}} ({{Legendary|★★★★}}), with both the rarity and the number of the stars marking the level of the non-optic attachments a weapon has. {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons whose attachments lack a {{Legendary}} version default to having {{Epic}} ones, for example an [[R-99 SMG|{{Legendary|R-99 SMG}}]] will have a {{ItemLink|Laser Sight|text={{Epic|Laser Sight}}}} equipped.

== Distribution ==
=== Battle Royale ===
In {{ItemLink|Battle Royale}}, all Fully Kitted Weapons are {{Legendary}}. Five weapons are selected each [[Season]] to be Fully Kitted Weapons, and have a 80% chance to spawn in Hot Zones marked on any [[map]], even if only one can spawn in a given Hot Zone. They can also spawn in map-specific loot sources such as gold [[loot ticks]] (1% chance), [[Flyers]] carrying gold death boxes (37.5% chance), gold [[cargo bots]] (1% chance) and [[loot MRVNs]] after giving them their missing arm (1% chance). Some very specific locations on certain maps also have a high chance of spawning a Fully Kitted Weapon.

=== Other modes ===
* In {{ItemLink|Control}}, all weapons are {{Rare}} Fully Kitted Weapons, and can be upgraded to {{Epic}} and then {{Legendary}} by earning enough Ratings to progress to the next Ratings Tier. Weapon ugrades can be skipped: for example, if a {{Rare}} weapon is equipped and {{Legendary|Ratings Tier 4}} is reached, that weapon will become {{Legendary}}.
* In {{ItemLink|Gun Run}}, all weapons are Fully Kitted Weapons, but their rarity varies depending on the weapon.
* In {{ItemLink|Team Deathmatch}}, all weapons are {{Rare}} Fully Kitted Weapons. At some point during the match, [[Care Package]]s containing two {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons and one {{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapon}} each start dropping into the arena.
* In {{ItemLink|Season 5}}'s [[Hunt]]s, specific {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons were available depending on the chapter.
* In [[Airdrop Escalation]], {{Rare}}, {{Epic}}, and {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons may be found inside Care Packages. The color of the Care Package beam changes accordingly based on which type of Fully Kitted Weapons it contains.
* In [[Armed and Dangerous]], {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted versions of certain [[sniper]]s and [[shotgun]]s may spawn rarely across the map.
* In [[Gold Rush Duos]], all available weapons are Seasonal {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons and {{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapons}}.
* In [[Deadeye]], all non-{{Mythic}} weapons are {{Legendary}} Fully-Kitted Weapons.

== Trivia ==
* Before {{ItemLink|Season 6}}, [[Supply Drop Weapon|{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapons}}]] were golden just like Fully Kitted Weapons. The term "gold weapon" could refer to either, and they both spawned in [[Gold Rush Duos]], a Limited Time Mode with only gold weapons. Respawn later upgraded the rarity of Supply Drop Weapons from {{Legendary}} to {{Mythic}} to differentiate the two, and now "gold weapon" is only used for Fully Kitted Weapons.
