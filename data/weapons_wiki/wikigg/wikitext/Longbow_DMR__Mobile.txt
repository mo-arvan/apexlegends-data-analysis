The '''Longbow DMR''', or '''Longbow''', is a [[designated marksman rifle]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Sniper Ammo}}.

The Longbow can be enhanced with the {{ItemLink|Skullpiercer Rifling|text={{Legendary|Skullpiercer Rifling}}}} Hop-Up which increases damage dealt by headshots.

== Pros and cons ==
; Pros
* Highest ammo capacity of all snipers.
* Can cause collateral shots, as projectiles are able to pierce through multiple enemies.
* Can open doors by shooting the handle.
* Highest fire rate of all sniper rifles.
*With instantly break shields with Skullpiercer equipped.

; Cons
* Poor hipfire accuracy.
* Strong recoil.
*Slower bullet speed and increased bullet drop compared to other sniper rifles.

== Lore ==
The '''D-101 Longbow DMR''' ([[wikipedia:Designated marksman rifle|'''D'''esignated '''M'''arksman '''R'''ifle]]) is a semi-automatic sniper rifle made by [[Lastimosa Armory]]. Models from the [[Frontier War]] were variants of the then-current R-201 Assault Rifle,<ref>[https://www.reddit.com/r/titanfall/comments/672pcz/i_framed_my_tier_1_diecast_tf2_weapons_shiny/dip21l7/ Comment by Ryan Lastimosa (brockhaurd) on r/titanfall]</ref> the model used in the [[Apex Games]] then would most likely be a variant of the R-201's successor, the [[R-301 Carbine]].

== Trivia ==
* Owing to its lineage as a variant of the [[R-301 Carbine]], it shares the same magazine design with said weapon, which is also the same model used for the {{ItemLink|Extended Light Mag|text=Extended Light Mags}}.
* Despite the fact that the Longbow is a designated marksman rifle (DMR), it is classified in-game as a [[sniper rifle]] instead of a [[marksman weapon]].
* The Longbow's {{Eternal|B-Savior}} skin may reference ''[[wikipedia:G-Saviour|G-Saviour]]'', a much-derided live-action adaptation of the ''[[wikipedia:Gundam|Gundam]]'' franchise.
