The '''Havoc Rifle''', or '''Havoc''', is a fully-automatic [[assault rifle]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Energy Ammo|text=Energy Ammo}}.

Every time the fire button is pressed, there is a spin-up delay before the weapon starts shooting. The spin-up delay can be removed by equipping a {{ItemLink|Turbocharger|text={{Legendary|Turbocharger}}}} Hop-Up.

== Pros And Cons ==
'''Pros'''

* Largest magazine capacity of all Assault Rifles at 26 rounds by default
* Same damage numbers as the Flatline.
* Similar iron sights to the Wingman and RE-45 Auto.
* Fastest TTK in the Assault Rifle category.

'''Cons'''

* Moderate fire-rate and a spin-up delay.
** Turbocharger Hop-Up is required to remove spin-up delay.
* Noticeably higher recoil than the Flatline.
** Unable to accept barrel mods.
* Uses a more uncommon ammo type, Energy Ammo, unlike the Flatline and Hemlok (which share Heavy Ammo), and the R-301 (which shares light ammo with several weapons.)
