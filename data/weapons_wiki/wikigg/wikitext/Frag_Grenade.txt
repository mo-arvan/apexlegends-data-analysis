The '''Frag Grenade''' is a [[grenade]] [[item]] which explodes and deals damage 4 seconds after being thrown. 

==Function==
When thrown, the Frag Grenade waits 4 seconds after being thrown to detonate, regardless of whether it bounces, rolls, or never touches a surface at all. The explosion has a radius of 8 meters, with a 2.4 meter inner radius where the Frag Grenade deals its maximum 100 damage - the further a target is from the inner radius, the less damage it deals. Directly hitting an enemy also deals an additional 10 damage. The explosion will also blast players away from itself. 

The Frag Grenade can be used to destroy doors. [[Fuse]]'s passive ability, {{AbilityLink|Grenadier}}, allows him to carry 2 Frag Grenades per inventory slot instead of 1.
