The '''L-STAR EMG''', or '''L-STAR''', is a fully-automatic [[energy machine gun]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Mythic Energy Ammo}}. It fires red plasma orb-like projectiles.

The L-STAR does not have a magazine, but instead draws ammo directly from the inventory. The L-STAR has a unique overheating mechanic, which triggers after {{Level0123|22|24|26|30}} continuous shots; it only needs to reload if it overheats.
