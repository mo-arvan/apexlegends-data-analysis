The '''{{PAGENAME}}''', also called '''Hemlok''', is an [[assault rifle]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Heavy Rounds}}.

Originally the [[Hemlok Burst AR]], the Hemlok Breach AR is a modified version from Ballistic's private elite arsenal. The main firing mode is <span class="invert">[[File:Auto Icon.svg|25px|Auto|link=]]</span> Full-auto, as opposed to the 3-round burst of the default variant. It features a built-in silencer, making it the only weapon to utilise one. The fire selector activates<span class="invert">[[File:Breach Charge Icon.svg|25px|Auto|link=]]</span> Breach Charge, a single-fire grenade launcher with 15 seconds cooldown. It deals 38 damage with a radial falloff and has a significant damage multiplier against [[Hardlight mesh]], structures (i.e. placeable objects like {{AbilityLink|Amped Cover}} or {{AbilityLink|Castle Wall}}), and [[Knockdown Shield]]s.
