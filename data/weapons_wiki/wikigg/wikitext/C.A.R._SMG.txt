The '''Combat Advanced Round SMG''' (shortened '''C.A.R. SMG''', '''C.A.R.''', or '''CAR''') is a fully-automatic [[submachine gun]] introduced in {{ItemLink|Season 11}}. By default it utilizes {{ItemLink|Heavy Rounds}}, but it can be toggled to accept {{ItemLink|Light Rounds}} instead by pressing {{KeySet|fireMode}}. The CAR can equip either an {{ItemLink|Extended Heavy Mag}} or an {{ItemLink|Extended Light Mag}}, regardless of which ammo type is currently in use; either will increase the magazine capacity in the same way.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|The C.A.R. SMG was developed by Lastimosa Armory to leverage the existing success of the company's R-101 platform. A smaller barrel, lighter alloys, and other weight reductions improve the weapon's handling, while smaller ammunition affords low recoil and a higher rate of fire. Traditionally, weapons with a high rate of fire tend to jam when used for extended durations - to remove this limitation, the R&D team at Lastimosa Armory created a "shoot by wire" system where the trigger is linked to the firing mechanism via an internal CPU. This system can detect overheat conditions, adjust rate of fire, and clear jams without operator intervention - often faster than the operator can realize a jam has occurred.}}

== Trivia ==
* Part of the C.A.R. SMG icon appeared in an UI asset showing the weapon's stock in the buy menu for {{ItemLink|Arenas}}, indicating that the weapon might've already had an icon stylized in the ''Apex'' style in {{ItemLink|Season 9}}.<ref>[https://twitter.com/alphaINTEL/status/1386724667130621956 Apex Legends News on Twitter]</ref> The icon can be seen in [https://media.contentapi.ea.com/content/dam/apex-legends/common/articles/introducing-arenas/apex-legends-arenas-blog-upgrade-weapon.mp4 a video] showing how to upgrade weapons in Arenas that got posted [https://www.ea.com/games/apex-legends/news/introducing-arenas on the official website].
* The C.A.R. had a defined recoil pattern way before its release.<ref>[https://twitter.com/kralrindo/status/1394768649416806403 KralRindo on Twitter - C.A.R. recoil pattern]</ref><ref>[https://twitter.com/kralrindo/status/1394768815431553026 KralRindo on Twitter - C.A.R. recoil pattern mid and close range comparison]</ref>
* The {{Legendary|Heaven Piercer}} skin likely references the anime series ''[[wikipedia:Gurren Lagann|Gurren Lagann]]''.
* A bespoke C.A.R., nicknamed "Lady Grey", was owned by [[Ballistic (character)|Ballistic]].<ref>[[Stories from the Outlands]] - [[Encore (Stories from the Outlands)|Encore]]</ref>
* The C.A.R. is one of two weapons (Bocek) to have a unique inspect animation when out of ammunition.
* The skeletons on the {{Legendary|Outlands Obliterator}} skin change their eye color to match the current ammo setting: Blue for {{ItemLink|Heavy Rounds|text=Heavy}} and orange for {{ItemLink|Light Rounds|text=Light}}.
