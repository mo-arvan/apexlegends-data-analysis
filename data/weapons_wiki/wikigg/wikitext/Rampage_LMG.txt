The '''Rampage LMG''', or '''Rampage''', is a fully-automatic [[light machine gun]] introduced in {{ItemLink|Season 10}} that utilizes {{ItemLink|Heavy Rounds}}. Its hipfire accuracy gradually increases the longer it is continuously fired.

The Rampage can be [[File:Heavy Rounds Revved Up.svg|22px]] Revved Up with a {{ItemLink|Thermite Grenade|text={{Common|Thermite Grenade}}}}, increasing the fire rate by 30% for 120 seconds, with each shot shortening the charge by ~0.6 seconds. Charging the Rampage takes 3.7 seconds, and will also reload the weapon upon a successful charge. In this state, the Rampage can also break doors in two shots, or one if the door was already damaged. The Rampage may also be enhanced with {{ItemLink|Gun Shield Generator|text={{Legendary|Gun Shield Generator}}}}, which creates a shield that absorbs up to 40 damage when aiming down sights with the weapon.

Being an LMG, the Rampage is affected by [[Rampart]]'s {{AbilityLink|Modded Loader}} Passive ability.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|It's like Ramya "Rampart" Parekh says: when you rise from the ashes of everything you've built, don't get mad - get heated. The Rampage LMG is the first gun she modded after a rival gang known as [[The Sisters]] burnt down her shop. Taking the [[M600 Spitfire]] as a base, it features a unique intake that can be loaded with a thermite canister to amp its fire rate. Inserting a thermite and overloading the firing mechanisms allows the user to deliver powerful shots (a distinct advantage over the base Spitfire) while increasing rate of fire for a set period of time. You could say it brings the heat.}}

== Trivia ==  
* The Rampage appeared in the [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeeCa18YK9M Season 8 Devstream]. When the explosive holds were being displayed, the weapon can be seen on one of the weapon racks using a very early model.
* If a Rampage has been Revved up before, then a spent Thermite Grenade will be present in the weapon when inspected, and the grenade will be ejected from the weapon at the start of the next Revved Up animation. This is true even if the weapon was Revved Up by another player.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBAY1VInFJM Mokeysniper on YouTube: Ejecting Thermites Out Of Rampage]</ref>
* [[Rampart]] has a unique animation for picking up the Rampage, where she loads the weapon more subtly than the other legends.
* The {{Cosmetic|3rd Anniversary|Epic|Crackling Surge}} skin for the Rampage was created as an homage to [https://www.youtube.com/c/TheGamingMerchant The Gaming Merchant], an ''Apex Legends'' YouTuber. The blue and gold color scheme references his thumbnail design, and his signature farewell "Cheerio" is written on the side.<ref>[https://twitter.com/PlayApex/status/1493647063082577923 Apex Legends on Twitter]</ref>
* The {{Cosmetic|Season 19|Legendary|Grace of Odin}} and {{Cosmetic|Season 19|Legendary|Wrath of Fenrir}} Rampage skins are revved up with a special {{ItemLink|Thermite Grenade}} skin. These, along with the {{Cosmetic|Season 17|Legendary|Frontier Faithful}} and {{Cosmetic|Season 17|Legendary|Outlands Obliterator}} C.A.R. skins are the only reactive weapon skins to have a special cosmetic of a gun attachment.
