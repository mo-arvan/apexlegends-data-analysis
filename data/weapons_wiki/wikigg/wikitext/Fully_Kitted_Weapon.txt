'''Fully Kitted Weapons''' are [[weapons]] equipped with all their possible [[attachments]] which cannot be removed, except for the [[Optic]]. If the weapon is dropped, the Optic stays on the weapon. There is no performance difference between a Fully Kitted Weapon and a weapon whose same attachments have been manually equipped.

Fully Kitted Weapons are tiered {{Common}} ({{Common|★}}), {{Rare}} ({{Rare|★★}}), {{Epic}} ({{Epic|★★★}}), and {{Legendary}} ({{Legendary|★★★★}}), with the rarity marking the level of the non-Optic attachments a weapon has. {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons whose attachments lack a {{Legendary}} version default to having {{Epic}} ones, for example an [[R-99 SMG|{{Legendary|R-99 SMG}}]] will have a {{ItemLink|Standard Stock|text={{Epic|Standard Stock}}}} equipped. As for the Optic, Fully Kitted Weapons carry the following:

* Assault Rifles are equipped with {{ItemLink|1x HCOG "Classic"|image=1x HCOG Classic.svg|text={{Common|1x HCOG "Classic"}}}} when {{Common}}, {{ItemLink|2x HCOG "Bruiser"|image=2x HCOG Bruiser.svg|text={{Rare|2x HCOG "Bruiser"}}}} otherwise;
* Submachine Guns are equipped with {{ItemLink|1x HCOG "Classic"|image=1x HCOG Classic.svg|text={{Common|1x HCOG "Classic"}}}};
* Light Machine Guns are equipped with {{ItemLink|1x HCOG "Classic"|image=1x HCOG Classic.svg|text={{Common|1x HCOG "Classic"}}}} when {{Common}}, {{ItemLink|2x HCOG "Bruiser"|image=2x HCOG Bruiser.svg|text={{Rare|2x HCOG "Bruiser"}}}} otherwise;
* Marksman Weapons are equipped with {{ItemLink|2x HCOG "Bruiser"|image=2x HCOG Bruiser.svg|text={{Rare|2x HCOG "Bruiser"}}}} when {{Common}} or {{Rare}}, {{ItemLink|2x-4x Variable AOG|text={{Epic|2x-4x Variable AOG}}}} otherwise;
* Sniper Rifles are equipped with {{ItemLink|6x Sniper|text={{Rare|6x Sniper}}}} when {{Common}} or {{Rare}}, {{ItemLink|4x-8x Variable Sniper|text={{Epic|4x-8x Variable Sniper}}}} otherwise;
* Shotguns are equipped with {{ItemLink|1x HCOG "Classic"|image=1x HCOG Classic.svg|text={{Common|1x HCOG "Classic"}}}};
* Pistols are equipped with {{ItemLink|1x HCOG "Classic"|image=1x HCOG Classic.svg|text={{Common|1x HCOG "Classic"}}}}.

Due to their different rarities coloring them with a specific color both outside and inside the inventory, Fully Kitted Weapons may also be referred to as {{Common|White}}, {{Rare|Blue}}, {{Epic|Purple}}, or {{Legendary|Gold}} Weapons.
<br>
<br>
== Distribution ==
=== Battle Royale ===
In {{ItemLink|Battle Royale}}, all Fully Kitted Weapons are {{Legendary}}. Five weapons are selected each [[Season]] to be Fully Kitted Weapons, and have a 80% chance to spawn in Hot Zones marked on any [[map]], even if only one can spawn in a given Hot Zone. They can also spawn in map-specific loot sources such as gold [[loot ticks]] (1% chance), [[Flyers]] carrying gold death boxes (37.5% chance), gold [[cargo bots]] (1% chance) and [[loot MRVNs]] after giving them their missing arm (1% chance). Some very specific locations on certain maps also have a high chance of spawning a Fully Kitted Weapon.

{{ItemLink|Ballistic|image=Ballistic Icon.svg}} can equip all weapons in his {{AbilityLink|Sling}}, including {{Legendary|Fully Kitted Weapons}}, but they will be converted into {{Common}} Fully Kitted variants and their optics cannot be removed. Sling weapons may be upgraded to {{Rare}} and {{Epic}} Fully Kitted variants at their respective levels. Removing the weapon from the Sling will return it to its previous state.

=== Other Modes ===
* In {{ItemLink|Control}}, all weapons are {{Rare}} Fully Kitted Weapons, and can be upgraded to {{Epic}} and then {{Legendary}} by earning enough Ratings to progress to the next Ratings Tier. Weapon ugrades can be skipped: for example, if a {{Rare}} weapon is equipped and {{Legendary|Ratings Tier 4}} is reached, that weapon will become {{Legendary}}.
* In {{ItemLink|Gun Run}}, all weapons are Fully Kitted Weapons, but their rarity varies depending on the weapon.
* In {{ItemLink|Team Deathmatch}}, all weapons are {{Rare}} Fully Kitted Weapons. At various points during the match, [[Care Package]]s containing two {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons and one [[Supply Drop Weapon|{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapon}}]] each start dropping into the arena.
* In {{ItemLink|Lockdown}}, all weapons are {{Rare}} Fully Kitted Weapons. When a Zone gets captured, two {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons and a {{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapon}} spawn in the middle of it.
* In {{ItemLink|Season 5}}'s [[Hunt]]s, specific {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons were available depending on the chapter.
* In [[Airdrop Escalation]], {{Rare}}, {{Epic}}, and {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons as well as [[Supply Drop Weapon|{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapons}}]] may be found inside [[Care Package]]s. The color of the Care Package beam changes accordingly based on which type of weapons it contains: blue, purple, gold, red.
* In [[Armed and Dangerous]], {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted versions of certain [[sniper]]s and [[shotgun]]s may spawn rarely across the map.
* In [[Gold Rush Duos]], all available weapons are Seasonal {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons and [[Supply Drop Weapon|{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapons}}]].
* In [[Quads]], {{Rare}} and {{Epic}} Fully Kitted Weapons may spawn in addition to Seasonal {{Legendary|Legendary}} ones.
* In [[Straight Shot]] and [[Solos]], all weapons are Fully Kitted, ranging from {{Common}} to {{Legendary}}.
* In [[TDM - Unshielded]], all weapons not in the {{Mythic|Care Package}} are {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons.
* During the [[Rift Relics Takeover]], some {{Legendary}} Fully Kitted Weapons statistically different than usual, called Relic Weapons, spawn before the Round 1 [[Ring]] starts closing.

== Trivia ==
* {{Epic|Pistols}} and {{Epic|Shotguns}} have their potentially compatible {{Legendary}} {{ItemLink|Hop-Up|text=Hop-Ups}}, despite being {{Epic}} Fully Kitted Weapons.
* Before {{ItemLink|Season 6}}, in {{ItemLink|Battle Royale}}, [[Supply Drop Weapon|{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapons}}]] had a golden color just like Fully Kitted Weapons. The term "gold weapon" could refer to either, and they both spawned in [[Gold Rush Duos]], a Limited Time Mode with only gold weapons. [[Respawn Entertainment|The developers]] later upgraded the rarity and color of Supply Drop Weapons from {{Legendary}} (gold) to {{Mythic}} (red) to differentiate the two.
