The '''Devotion LMG''', or '''Devotion''', is a fully-automatic [[light machine gun]] that utilizes Energy Ammo.

As a Supply Drop Weapon, it can only be found in Care Packages, but is equipped with the highest tier of attachments available, including a Barrel Stabilizer, Energy Mag, Standard Stock, 2x HCOG "Bruiser", and Turbocharger.

It has a unique RPM acceleration mechanic: the longer the Devotion is fired continuously, the faster its fire rate will become, up to a cap. The {{ItemLink|Turbocharger|text={{Legendary|Turbocharger}}}} Hop-Up allows the Devotion to reach its highest fire rate faster. As a Supply Drop Weapon, it can charge up even more quickly and skips some of the initial rev.

== Pros And Cons ==
'''Pros'''

* Larger magazine than the Spitfire.
* Low bullet drop
* Excellent damage output.

'''Cons'''

* Moderate recoil
* RPM accelerates overtime, requiring a few seconds of sustained fire to achieve maximum fire-rate.
