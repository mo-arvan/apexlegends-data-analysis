== Pros and Cons ==
'''Pros'''

* Fastest reload time of all Assault Rifles
* Recoil is minimal and mostly kicks upwards.
* Highest fire-rate of all Assault Rifles, faster than some SMGs.
* Ability to swap between Auto and Semi-Auto for precision shots.
** Recoil is rendered nonexistent in Semi-Auto.

'''Cons'''

* Lowest damage of all Assault Rifles, comparable to some SMGs.
* Four attachment slots, making fully kitting less convenient.
* Semi-Auto is a slower-firing Auto mode.
* Smallest magazine of all Assault Rifles by default, at 20 rounds.
