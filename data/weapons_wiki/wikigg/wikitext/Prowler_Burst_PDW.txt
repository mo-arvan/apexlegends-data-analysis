The '''Prowler Burst PDW''', '''Prowler SMG''', or '''Prowler''', is a [[submachine gun]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Heavy Rounds}}. It fires in a 5-round burst.

It is equipped with a {{ItemLink|Selectfire Receiver|image=Selectfire Receiver.svg|text={{Legendary|Selectfire Receiver}}}}, which allows the Prowler to switch to full-auto mode. 375 Locked Hop-Up Points are required to unlock the {{ItemLink|Selectfire Receiver|image=Selectfire Receiver.svg|text={{Legendary|Selectfire Receiver}}}}.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|Designed for personal defense, the Prowler Burst PDW was an essential firearm for Pilots whose Titans were compromised during battle. Its compact shape gives it the punch of an assault rifle at a much smaller size, and its burst-action design allows the operator to lay down significant cover-fire - this was particularly helpful for Pilots ejecting from their Titans. It can also be upgraded to a full-auto mode that allows the operator to put heavy fire down-range with the effective accuracy of an assault rifle.}}

== Trivia ==
* The Prowler is based on two real [[wikipedia:personal defense weapon|personal defense weapons]]: the [[wikipedia:FN P90|FN P90]] and the [[wikipedia:Magpul PDR|Magpul PDR-C]]. It's also an homage to Seburo, Masamune Shirow's fictional arms manufacturer from ''Ghost in the Shell'' and ''Appleseed''.
* The Prowler's icon lacks iron sights.
