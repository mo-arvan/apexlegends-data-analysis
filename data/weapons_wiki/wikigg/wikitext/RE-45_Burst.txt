The '''RE-45 Burst''' is a burst-firing [[pistol]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Energy Ammo}}.

Originally the [[RE-45 Auto]], the RE-45 Burst is a modified energy ammo version from Ballistic's private elite arsenal. The weapon features a small windup similar to that of the [[HAVOC Rifle]], before firing in 3-round bursts with an auto-trigger similar to that of the [[Nemesis Burst AR]].

As of {{SeasonLink|28}} Split 2, this became the default RE-45, with the RE-45 Auto effectively retired from the game. This also removed the Elite Weapon "Hop-Up" and graphic in the inventory.
