The '''Kraber .50-Cal Sniper''', or '''Kraber''', is a bolt-action [[sniper rifle]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Mythic Sniper Ammo}}.

Being a [[Supply Drop Weapon|{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapon}}]] it can only be found in [[Care Package]]s or [[Supply Bin|{{Mythic|Mythic Supply Bins}}]] and cannot equip foreign ammo, having 12 total shots at its disposal. It also comes pre-equipped with a unique {{Mythic|6x-10x Variable Sniper}} optic.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|The original [[tf:Kraber-AP Sniper|Kraber-AP sniper rifle]] was designed to take down slow-moving military vehicles. Its successor, the Kraber .50-Cal, packs the same kind of power against much smaller targets. A single long-range shot can down a human target. A close-range shot, while significantly harder to land, will obliterate them.}}

== Trivia ==
* The Kraber is named after Erik Kraber, a Senior Audio Director at [[Respawn Entertainment]].
* The Kraber is the only weapon to have never been removed from the [[Care Package]].
* Despite being a [[Supply Drop Weapon|{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapon}}]], the Kraber doesn't have any pre-equipped attachment besides its unique optic.
