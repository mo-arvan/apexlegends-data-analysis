The '''Triple Take''' is a triple-barreled [[marksman rifle]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Energy Ammo}}. It fires 3 projectiles, one at the center and two to its sides, and consumes 3 ammo each shot. The spread is gradually reduced while aiming down sights when using the choke fire mode ({{ItemLink|Choke Active Icon|noText=1}}).

== Pros and cons ==
; Pros
* High theoretical damage output.
* High projectile speed.
* Low projectile drop.
*Good hipfire performance due to several factors.
** Fires 3 projectiles in a horizontal spread, which provides a higher chance of damaging evasive targets.
**Low bullet spread.

; Cons
* Slowest fire rate out of all marksman weapons.
**Fully choked shots are required for maximum damage at long range. As a result, the fire rate is further reduced.
* Damage is split over 3 projectiles.
* Consumes 3 {{ItemLink|Energy Ammo}} per shot.
