The '''Mozambeam''' is an April Fools' variant of the [[Mozambique Shotgun]] introduced on [[April Fools' Day (2023)|2023's April Fools' Day]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Energy Ammo}}. It fires three hitscan beams in the shape of a triangle after a short delay, consuming 3 ammo with each shot. The Mozambeam essentially acts like the now removed [[HAVOC Rifle]]'s single-fire mode.

Being a shotgun, the Mozambeam affects [[Mad Maggie]]'s movement speed because of her {{AbilityLink|Warlord's Ire}} Passive ability.

Mozambeam utilizes the {{Epic|Off the Grid}} skin by default, but it can be converted into the equipped skin and Charm combination the player is currently using for the Mozambique.

This is one of the seven April Fools' weapons in ''Apex Legends'', the other six being: [[Mozambique Shotgun/April Fools'|April Fools' Mozambique Shotgun]], [[P2020/April Fools'|April Fools' P2020]], [[Mozamblam]], [[Mozambrrr]], [[Mozamburst]], and [[Ol' Nessie]].
