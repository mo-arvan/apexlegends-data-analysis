== Lore ==
The '''A-13 Sentry''' is a prototype sniper rifle made by [[Paradinha Arsenal]] and was the basis for the development of the [[Sentinel|Sentinel ESR]].

== Trivia ==
* Unlike its successor, the [[Sentinel|Sentinel ESR]], the Sentry has the ability to split into multiple segments for storage. 
* The Sentry was gifted to Vantage by her mother, Xenia Contreras, as a birthday gift. 
* Xenia claimed it was a family heirloom. Contrary to this, however, the Sentry was simply the standard weapon aboard the GDS ''Vantage'', the prison ship she had been trapped on.
