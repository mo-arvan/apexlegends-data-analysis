== Pros and Cons ==
; Pros
* High fire rate, and automatic.
* Controllable and predictable recoil.
* Excellent hipfire performance.
* Minimal movement penalty when aiming (5%).

; Cons
* Low damage per shot.
* Low DPS.
* Small magazine capacity for an automatic weapon.
**Not ammo-efficient.
* 1× headshot multiplier at 38+ meters.
