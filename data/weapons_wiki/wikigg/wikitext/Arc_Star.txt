The '''Arc Star''' is a [[grenade]] [[item]] which sticks to surfaces or enemies and explodes, dealing damage and slowing enemies. 

==Function==
When thrown, the Arc Star waits 2.8 seconds to detonate after sticking to a surface or enemy. The explosion has a radius of 8.75 meters, with a 1.8 meter inner radius where the Arc Star deals its maximum 75 damage - the further a target is from the inner radius, the less damage it deals. Directly sticking an enemy also deals an additional 10 damage. The Arc Star also causes blurred vision and slow movement speed, which lasts a maximum of 5 seconds, decreasing with distance from the inner radius. The explosion also reduces momentum of airborne or sliding enemies.

The Arc Star can be used to destroy [[door]]s. It can also disable a [[Trident]] for 5 seconds, if it is stuck or caught in its blast radius. The Arc Star will bounce off of squadmates, as well as shields like the {{ItemLink|Knockdown Shield}}, [[Gibraltar]]'s {{AbilityLink|Gun Shield}}, or [[Newcastle]]'s {{AbilityLink|Mobile Shield}}. The explosion consumes the cooldown on [[Ash]]'s dash and [[Sparrow's]] double jump.

[[Fuse]]'s passive ability, {{AbilityLink|Grenadier}}, allows him to carry 2 Arc Stars per inventory slot instead of 1.
