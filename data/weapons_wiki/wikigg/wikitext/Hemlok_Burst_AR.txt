The '''Hemlok Burst AR''', also called '''Hemlok''', is an [[assault rifle]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Heavy Rounds}}. It may be switched between 3-shot burst and semi-automatic fire modes.

With the introduction of the [[Hemlok Breach AR]] in {{SeasonLink|28}} Split 2, this variant is currently unavailable.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|The Hemlok Burst AR is a heavy burst-fire assault rifle based on the Hemlok BF-R, a weapon designed exclusively for IMC ground troops. Lastimosa Armory was commissioned by the IMC to create a weapon with a stronger punch than the R-101, so it could more effectively puncture standard-issue body armor. During the Frontier War, direct sale to the public was prohibited, but its updated model is commonplace in the Outlands.}}
