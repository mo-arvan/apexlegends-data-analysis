The '''Thermite Grenade''' is a [[grenade]] [[item]] which creates a streak of fire across the ground. 

==Function==
When thrown, the Thermite Grenade travels and bounces off of walls until it hits a floor. At that point, it creates a 6 meter long line of flame across the ground, perpendicular to the direction it was thrown from, which lasts 8 seconds. Enemies take 4 damage per tick (8 damage/sec) when standing in the flames, and receive an afterburn effect which deals 25 damage whether or not they leave the flames. 

The Thermite Grenade will deal damage to [[door]]s. A Thermite Grenade can be expended to rev up the [[Rampage LMG]], boosting its fire rate. [[Fuse]]'s passive ability, {{AbilityLink|Grenadier}}, allows him to carry 2 Thermite Grenades per inventory slot instead of 1.
