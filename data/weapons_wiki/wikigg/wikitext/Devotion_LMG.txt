The '''Devotion LMG''', or '''Devotion''', is a fully-automatic [[light machine gun]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Energy Ammo}}. The longer it is fired continuously, the faster its fire rate and hipfire accuracy increase, up to a cap. 

It is equipped with a {{ItemLink|Turbocharger|text={{Legendary|Turbocharger}}}}, which drastically reduces the amount of time to reach the maximum rate of fire. The {{ItemLink|Turbocharger|text={{Legendary|Turbocharger}}}} requires 600 Locked Hop-Up Points to activate.
<!--Being a [[Supply Drop Weapon|{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapon}}]], the Devotion can only be found in [[Care Package]]s, is equipped with all of its highest rarity [[attachments]], and cannot equip foreign ammo, having 324 total shots at its disposal. It also comes pre-equipped with {{ItemLink|Turbocharger|text={{Legendary|Turbocharger}}}}, which causes the RPM acceleration to reach maximum faster, and {{ItemLink|Gun Shield Generator|text={{Legendary|Gun Shield Generator}}}}, which creates a shield that absorbs up to 40 damage while aiming down sights with the weapon.-->

Being an LMG, the Devotion is affected by [[Rampart]]'s {{AbilityLink|Modded Loader}} Passive ability.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|The Devotion demands something different from its handler than most weapons: commitment. A cyclical, energy-assisted firing action increases its rate of fire the longer its trigger is held. This added "spin-up" time can make the Devotion a risky choice in heated situations. However, upgrading it with a Turbocharger hop-up decreases its spin-up significantly and makes the fealty worthwile.}}
