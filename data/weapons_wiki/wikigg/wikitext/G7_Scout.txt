== Lore ==
{{Quote|The G7 Scout comes from good stock. The successor to the [[tf:G2A4 Battle Rifle|G2A4 Assault Rifle]] and the [[tf:G2A5 Battle Rifle|G2A5 Battle Rifle]], the G7 features a simple design and parts that are easy to reproduce, making it a crowd favorite in the Outlands' most remote regions. The Scout adds a new feature to the family with its updated sniper stock, improving long-range accuracy while not sacrificing mid-range effectiveness.}}

The G2A6, later renamed to the G7 Scout, has been simplified from its design in ''Titanfall 2''. Based on the M14/M1A rifle platform, the G7's drop in frame was replaced from a milled tactical stock to a solid composite body intended to serve the role as a Scout Sniper Rifle. This weapon uses 6.19x97mm LEC bullets that gives it quite a punch at mid range to long distance. When used as a DMR, your team can effectively advance and maneuver using your sight acquisition as a base of fire. It's overall mechanics haven't changed much throughout the years, as its reliability and brutal precision is a testament to its real-world inspiration.<ref name="ArtStation Lastimosa">[https://www.artstation.com/artwork/YaRLPX Ryan Lastimosa on ArtStation]</ref>

== Trivia ==
* The G7 Scout is the one of the two weapons (Other is [[Nemesis Burst AR]] to have a rare reload animation, where the empty magazine may be ejected and then a new one is inserted both from the top. This particular reload is a reference to the [[wikipedia:M1 Garand|M1 Garand]], which the [[wikipedia:M14 rifle|M14]] and, by extension, the G7 Scout, are based on.
* The G7 Scout is the only weapon whose classification changed twice, first from sniper rifle to assault rifle in {{ItemLink|Season 4}}, and then from assault rifle to marksman weapon in {{ItemLink|Season 9}}.
* It was originally designated as the G2A6, making it more in line with the naming scheme of its [[Frontier War]] ancestors.
* The G7 Scout appearance is based on the real [[wikipedia:Springfield Armory M1A#SOCOM rifles|Springfield Armory M1A SOCOM 16]] semi-automatic rifle fitted with a [http://www.jallenglobal.com/jae-100-g3-standard-orders/ J. Allen Enterprises JAE-100 chassis].
* {{Legendary|The Crimson Firedrake}} skin sports the phrase "You Owe Me Awe", a nod to the 1986 thriller ''[[wikipedia:Manhunter (film)|Manhunter]]'', in which the main antagonist Francis Dolarhyde says this line to his victim Freddy Lounds before murdering him.
* The G7 Scout is [[Bangalore]]'s favorite weapon. It is seen in a few of her banner poses, her character select animation, and her lobby animations.<ref>[[Pathfinder's Quest]]</ref>
* The {{Cosmetic|season 6|Legendary|Supersonic}} and {{Cosmetic|season 6|Legendary|Hypersonic}} skins are weapon mods designed by [[Rampart]], indicated by the presence of a Rampart Mods stamp.
** While the {{Cosmetic|Death By Design|Legendary|Crested Vanguard}} is based on the same model, it does not carry the Rampart Mods stamp. It instead has Belva's Lion crest in its place, possibly indicating a collaboration between the two.
