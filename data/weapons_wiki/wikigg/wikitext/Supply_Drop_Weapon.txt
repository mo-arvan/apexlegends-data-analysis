'''Supply Drop Weapons''' are [[weapon]]s that can only be found in [[Care Package]]s. Being essentially [[Fully Kitted Weapon]]s, they are equipped with all their best [[attachments]] which cannot be removed, except for the [[Optic]]. If the weapon is dropped, the Optic still stays on the weapon. Supply Drop Weapons may also receive adequate improvements, whether through direct changes to their statistics or through {{ItemLink|Hop-Up|image=Hop Up Slot.svg|text=Hop-Ups}}, in order to make them stronger than when they were on [[Loot|ground loot]].

Supply Drop Weapons always come in {{Mythic}} rarity.

== Trivia ==
* The [[Kraber]] has never left the Care Package.
* Before {{ItemLink|Season 6}}, in {{ItemLink|Battle Royale}}, {{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapons}} had a golden color just like Fully Kitted Weapons. The term "gold weapon" could refer to either, and they both spawned in [[Gold Rush Duos]], a Limited Time Mode with only gold weapons. [[Respawn Entertainment|The developers]] later upgraded the rarity and of Supply Drop Weapons from {{Legendary}} (gold) to {{Mythic}} (red) to differentiate the two.
