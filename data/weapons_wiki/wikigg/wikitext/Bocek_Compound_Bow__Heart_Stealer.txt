The '''Heart Stealer''' is a variant of the [[Bocek Compound Bow]] introduced in {{ItemLink|Season 15}} that utilizes {{ItemLink|Mythic Arrows}}, exclusive to the [[Date Night]] Limited Time Takeover. It only spawns in specific spots of the map, marked by a magenta beam, pointing at a [[Loot Tick]]. Breaking the Tick will drop a Heart Stealer. If a [[Care Package]] would spawn a Bocek, it spawns a Heart Stealer instead.

The Heart Stealer is functionally identical to the Bocek. However, dealing damage to enemy players will heal the player wielding it by half the damage dealt. If a player's teammate is inside the magenta circle created whenever the Heart Stealer is equipped, they also get healed.

Being a [[Supply Drop Weapon|{{Mythic|Supply Drop Weapon}}]], it is equipped with all of its highest rarity [[attachments]], and cannot equip foreign ammo. It also comes pre-equipped with 60 {{ItemLink|Mythic Arrows|text=Ammo}}. Due to the pre-equipped {{ItemLink|Shatter Caps|text={{Epic|Shatter Caps}}}}, hipfiring the Heart Stealer allows it to fire multiple arrows similarly to shotguns.

The Heart Stealer utilizes the {{Cosmetic|Valentine's Day 2023|Epic|Cupid's Arrow}} skin and the {{Cosmetic|Valentine's Day 2020|Epic|Love Struck}} {{ItemLink|Charm|text=Weapon Charm}} by default, but it can be converted into the equipped skin and Charm combination currently in use for the Bocek.
