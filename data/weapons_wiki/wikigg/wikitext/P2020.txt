The '''P2020''' is a semi-automatic [[pistol]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Mythic Light Rounds}}. A second P2020 may be equipped in the same weapon slot, enabling the [[P2020#Akimbo|Akimbo]] state. <!--The P2020 can be enhanced with the {{ItemLink|Hammerpoint Rounds|text={{Legendary|Hammerpoint Rounds}}}} Hop-Up, which increases damage done to unshielded targets by 10%.-->

The P2020 has an April Fools' [[P2020/April Fools|enhanced version]].
<br>
<br>
== Akimbo ==
The P2020 can become '''Akimbo''' by equipping two P2020s in the same weapon slot. While Akimbo, the two P2020s:

* are fully automatic;
* have their fire rate increased from 420 to 480 RPM;
* have their total magazine size doubled from {{Level0123|8|9|10|11}} to {{Level0123|16|18|20|22}};
* have their tactical reload speed increased from 1.25 to 2.1 seconds;
* have their full reload speed increased from 1.25 to 2.6 seconds;
* share any attachment they have equipped without the need to search for another;
* cannot be aimed down sights with, as attempting to do so will instead tighten their hipfire spread, and their Optics are disabled;
* share any skin currently equipped on the base P2020.

The Akimbo state can be toggled on/off by pressing {{KeySet|fireMode}}, without the need to drop the second P2020. Toggling Akimbo off will return the P2020 to its original single-weapon state.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|This small but reliable pistol was once standard-issue for IMC recruits before the Blackout. While lacking significant firepower, it's famously accurate with limited recoil, and modifications can significantly increase its rate of fire. A stand-off device in the muzzle also ensures greater accuracy in close quarters. When all other magazines are spent, it's comforting to have a P2020 on your hip.}}

The Hammond P2020, is a semi-automatic single-action small caliber pistol manufactured by [[Lastimosa Armory]]. This handgun features an integrated standoff device and a fully milled slide. It also has a fully integrated Taclight and LAM, along with varied functions for IR, LiDAR, or FLIR illumination. When dropping in hot and you need something more than your mitts, the P2020 will help you tap, rack, and bang to first place with the best [[Legends]] out there.

== Trivia ==
* Its predecessors, the '''P2011''' and '''P2016''', were references to the year development of ''Titanfall'' and ''Titanfall 2'''s release year respectively.  Following the naming scheme, the P2020 was supposed to be the P2019 but was changed before release to its current name.
** Some P2020 skins reference this, having "P2019" labeled on the weapon instead of "P2020".
* [[Ballistic]], [[Crypto]], [[Mirage]], and [[Alter]] have some unique animation for {{PAGENAME}} as following:
** At end of single {{PAGENAME}} inspection, Ballistic does the press check on it.
** Ballistic, Crypto, and Mirage have {{PAGENAME}} Akimbo tac-reload and full-reload animations.
** Mirage has his own {{PAGENAME}} Akimbo inspection animation, with voicelines.
** Alter has her own pickup 2nd {{PAGENAME}} or {{PAGENAME}} Akimbo animation.
