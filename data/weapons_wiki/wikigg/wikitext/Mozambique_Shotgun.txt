The '''Mozambique Shotgun''', or '''Mozambique''', is a fully-automatic triple-barreled [[shotgun pistol]] that utilizes {{ItemLink|Shotgun Shells}}. It fires three pellets in the shape of a triangle. A second Mozambique may be equipped in the same weapon slot, enabling the [[Mozambique Shotgun#Akimbo|Akimbo]] state.
<!--The Mozambique can be enhanced with the {{ItemLink|Hammerpoint Rounds|text={{Legendary|Hammerpoint Rounds}}}} Hop-Up, which increases damage done to unshielded targets by 20%.-->

Being a shotgun, the Mozambique affects [[Mad Maggie]]'s movement speed because of her {{AbilityLink|Warlord's Ire}} Passive ability.

Six April Fools' variants exist for the Mozambique: an [[/April Fools|enhanced version]], [[Ol' Nessie]], [[Mozambrrr]], [[Mozamburst]], [[Mozambeam]], and [[Mozamblam]].
<br>
<br>

== Akimbo ==
The Mozambique can become '''Akimbo''' by equipping two Mozambiques in the same weapon slot. While Akimbo, the two Mozambiques:

* have their fire rate increased from {{Level0123|160|176|184|192}} to {{Level0123|175|193|201|210}} RPM;
* have their total magazine size doubled from 5 to 10;
* have their tactical reload speed increased from 2.1 to 2.5 seconds;
* have their full reload speed increased from 2.6 to 3 seconds;
* share any attachment they have equipped without the need to search for another;
* cannot be aimed down sights with as attempting to do so will instead tighten their hipfire spread, and their Optics are disabled;
* share any skin currently equipped on the base Mozambique.

The Akimbo state can be toggled on/off by pressing {{KeySet|fireMode}}, without the need to drop the second Mozambique. Toggling Akimbo off will return the Mozambique to its original single-weapon state.

== Lore ==
{{Quote|In the wake of the mass Frontier migration into the Outlands, regional conflicts and land-grabs created a demand for inexpensive firearms. The Mozambique Shotgun (named for [[wikipedia:Mozambique Drill|an execution technique with a similar, triangular spread]]) served that function for Pilots in the Frontier War and a new model was soon being mass-produced for civilian use. The result is a firearm with a small magazine and minimal stopping power - while useful for self-defense, a standard Mozambique will require modifications (or a lot of luck) to be useful in a real fight.}}

== Trivia ==
* The name and triangular pellet pattern of the Mozambique Shotgun is a reference to the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozambique_Drill Mozambique Drill] technique of shooting someone twice in the chest and once in the head.
** [[Bangalore]] may reference this when [[Bangalore/Voice_lines#Pings|pinging]] a Mozambique.
** The Mozambique Drill is also referenced with an {{ItemLink|Anniversary}} event challenge of the same name.
* Requesting optics for the Mozambique will result in the Legend asking for pistol optics, instead of shotgun ones, despite the weapon being classified as a shotgun.
** The Mozambique being a pistol was also further supported by {{ItemLink|Hammerpoint Rounds|text={{Legendary|Hammerpoint Rounds}}}}, which are shown to be compatible with "pistols, except the [[Wingman]]".
* Since the game has been released, the Mozambique has become a popular [[wikipedia:meme|meme]] among players due to it being hardly ever useful in fights because of the low damage output and the very slow time to kill; the weapon is also memed for the line ''Mozambique here!'', said by all [[Legends]] when pinging one, with players especially mimicking [[Lifeline]] because of her unique accent. Even after the various buffs the Mozambique received through the [[Seasons]] to the point of becoming a far more viable weapon than it was originally, the meme is still popular to this day.
** [[Respawn Entertainment]] themselves officially acknowledged the existence of this meme during the second {{ItemLink|Anniversary}} of the game, by adding a {{ItemLink|Charm|text=Weapon Charm}} as well as a {{ItemLink|Badge}}.
** During April Fools' 2023, pinging any weapon (except the Mozambique) would transorm it into a random April Fools' variant of the Mozambique based on its original ammo type, essentially rendering impossible to ping other weapons than the Mozambique. During the same say, most [[Map Features]] such as [[Door]]s and [[Zipline]]s also served the same fate, replacing themselves with Mozambiques once pinged.
** When Lifeline's special inspect animation with akimbo Mozambiques is triggered, she references the meme.
* During the {{ItemLink|Preseason}} and {{ItemLink|Season 1}}, the Mozambique had a unique drop animation where the Legends would forcefully throw it on the ground instead of simply letting the weapon fall. It has been removed in {{ItemLink|Season 2}}.
* Lifeline, [[Mad Maggie]], and [[Alter]] have some unique animations for the Mozambique as following:
** Maggie has Mozambique akimbo tactical reload and full reload animations.
** Lifeline has a unique inspect animation for akimbo Mozambiques, referencing the popular meme involving her and the gun.
** Alter has her own second Mozambique pickup or Mozambique Akimbo animation, using her cybernetic tail to pick up the second gun, as well as an occasional voiceline.
